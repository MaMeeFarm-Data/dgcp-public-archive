# DGCP™ Daily Public Signal

## 211: System-of-Systems

### Observation

System-of-systems continues describing environments in which multiple independently functioning systems interact, exchange resources or information, and contribute to broader functions that extend beyond the operation of any single system.

These environments can include physical infrastructure, digital networks, organizations, institutions, services, supply systems, and other interconnected structures that retain distinct functions while participating in larger operational relationships.

### DGCP™ Insight

System-of-systems continues reflecting how independently functioning systems can become interconnected through shared functions, resources, information, services, and operational relationships.

---

## 212: Cross-System Dependencies

### Observation

Cross-system dependencies continue forming where the operation of one system relies on resources, services, infrastructure, information, capabilities, or functions provided by another system.

These dependencies can extend across sectors, organizations, technologies, geographic areas, and institutional boundaries through shared infrastructure, service relationships, information flows, resource networks, and operational connections.

### DGCP™ Insight

Cross-system dependencies continue connecting distinct systems through relationships in which functions and capabilities depend on resources, services, information, or infrastructure provided elsewhere.

---

## 213: Structural Understanding

### Observation

Structural understanding continues developing through the systematic observation of components, relationships, boundaries, interfaces, dependencies, capacities, resources, controls, evidence, and operational interactions across interconnected systems.

Understanding these structures depends on maintaining clear distinctions among observed conditions, documented relationships, system capabilities, operational states, and interpretations derived from available evidence.

### DGCP™ Insight

Structural understanding continues connecting observations of system components and relationships into coherent representations of how interconnected systems are organized and operate.
