# DGCP™ Daily Public Signal

## 223: Shared Infrastructure

### Observation

Shared infrastructure continues supporting multiple systems, organizations, services, users, or operational functions through common physical or digital assets and networks.

Shared infrastructure can include transportation networks, energy systems, telecommunications, computing environments, facilities, utilities, and other assets whose capabilities are used across multiple participating functions or systems.

### DGCP™ Insight

Shared infrastructure continues connecting multiple systems and functions through common physical and digital assets that support interconnected activity.

---

## 224: Shared Services

### Observation

Shared services continue providing common functions or capabilities that can be used across multiple systems, organizations, departments, or operational environments.

Shared services can include information systems, communications, computing, logistics, administrative functions, technical support, financial services, identity systems, and other capabilities organized to support multiple participating functions.

### DGCP™ Insight

Shared services continue supporting interconnected operation by providing common capabilities across multiple systems, organizations, and operational functions.

---

## 225: Shared Capacity

### Observation

Shared capacity continues representing operational capability that is available to support multiple systems, services, organizations, users, or functions rather than being dedicated exclusively to a single activity.

Shared capacity can depend on infrastructure, resources, allocation mechanisms, access arrangements, scheduling, technical compatibility, service requirements, coordination, and the demands placed on available capability by participating functions.

### DGCP™ Insight

Shared capacity continues reflecting how available operational capability can be distributed and used across multiple interconnected functions and systems.
