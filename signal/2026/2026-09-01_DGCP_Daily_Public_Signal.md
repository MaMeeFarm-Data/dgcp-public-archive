# DGCP™ Daily Public Signal

## 124  System Architecture

### Observation

System architecture continues defining how components, functions, interfaces, information flows, and operational dependencies are organized within complex systems.

Architectural structures connect physical infrastructure, digital systems, processes, institutions, resources, and human capabilities through defined relationships and interfaces.

### DGCP™ Insight

System architecture continues providing structural organization through which interconnected components and functions operate as part of a larger system.

---

## 125  Functional Layers

### Observation

Functional layers continue organizing complex systems into distinct but interconnected areas of capability, responsibility, and operation.

Individual layers can perform different functions while remaining dependent on information, resources, interfaces, infrastructure, and services provided by other layers.

### DGCP™ Insight

Functional layers continue supporting structured system operation by separating distinct functions while maintaining relationships across interconnected layers.

---

## 126  Structural Relationships

### Observation

Structural relationships continue connecting components, layers, organizations, infrastructure, resources, and processes across complex systems.

These relationships can include dependencies, interfaces, resource flows, information exchanges, authority structures, and operational connections that influence how system components function together.

### DGCP™ Insight

Structural relationships continue shaping system organization through the connections and dependencies that link individual components into larger operational structures.
