# DGCP™ Daily Public Signal

## 67 — Labor Markets

### Observation

Labor markets continue connecting workers, employers, skills, occupations, and economic activity across industries and regions.

Labor market activity depends on interconnected businesses, institutions, education systems, workforce participation, compensation structures, regulation, and broader economic conditions.

## 69 — Human Capital

### Observation

Human capital reflects accumulated knowledge, skills, experience, and capabilities that individuals contribute to economic and organizational activity.

Its development remains connected to education, training, health, professional experience, institutional environments, and opportunities to apply and extend existing capabilities.

### DGCP™ Insight

Human capital remains structurally connected to system capability through the knowledge, skills, experience, and capabilities embodied in people.
