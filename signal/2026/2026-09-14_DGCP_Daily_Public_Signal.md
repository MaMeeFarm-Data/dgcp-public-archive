# DGCP™ Daily Public Signal

## 163: Recovery Systems

### Observation

Recovery systems continue providing organized structures, capabilities, resources, and procedures for restoring affected functions, services, infrastructure, or operations following disruption or loss of capability.

These systems can depend on monitoring, defined responsibilities, communication, technical resources, alternative arrangements, operational coordination, documented procedures, and access to supporting infrastructure and services.

### DGCP™ Insight

Recovery systems continue providing structured mechanisms through which affected functions and services can be restored following disruption.

---

## 164: Recovery Capacity

### Observation

Recovery capacity continues representing the available capability of systems and organizations to restore affected functions, services, infrastructure, or operational activity following disruption.

Recovery capacity can depend on personnel, equipment, materials, information, financial resources, technical capability, backup capacity, infrastructure access, coordination, and the availability of supporting services.

### DGCP™ Insight

Recovery capacity continues reflecting the resources and capabilities available to support the restoration of affected system functions and operations.

---

## 165: Restoration Processes

### Observation

Restoration processes continue organizing the activities through which affected components, services, infrastructure, and operational functions are returned to required operating conditions.

These processes can include assessment, coordination, resource deployment, repair, replacement, reconfiguration, verification, and the progressive re-establishment of system functions and connections.

### DGCP™ Insight

Restoration processes continue connecting recovery capabilities with coordinated activities that return affected functions and services to required operating conditions.
