# DGCP™ Daily Public Signal

## 172: Concentration

### Observation

Concentration continues occurring where resources, capabilities, activities, services, infrastructure, or system functions are distributed unevenly and become clustered within a limited number of components, locations, organizations, or network positions.

Concentration can emerge through system design, economic organization, infrastructure development, resource distribution, operational requirements, network structures, and institutional arrangements.

### DGCP™ Insight

Concentration continues shaping system structure by clustering resources, capabilities, functions, and activities within particular parts of interconnected systems.

---

## 173: Geographic Concentration

### Observation

Geographic concentration continues occurring where infrastructure, production, resources, services, capabilities, or operational activities are located within a limited number of geographic areas.

Its structure can depend on resource availability, infrastructure access, transportation networks, energy systems, workforce distribution, market access, environmental conditions, historical development, and institutional arrangements.

### DGCP™ Insight

Geographic concentration continues reflecting how system capabilities, resources, and activities are distributed across physical locations and regions.

---

## 174: Capacity Concentration

### Observation

Capacity concentration continues occurring where a significant share of available production, processing, storage, transportation, computing, service, or other operational capability is located within a limited number of facilities, organizations, nodes, or geographic areas.

The structure of capacity concentration can depend on infrastructure requirements, economies of scale, resource access, network connectivity, technology, capital deployment, operational configuration, and the distribution of supporting systems.

### DGCP™ Insight

Capacity concentration continues reflecting how operational capability is distributed across facilities, organizations, network nodes, and geographic areas within interconnected systems.
