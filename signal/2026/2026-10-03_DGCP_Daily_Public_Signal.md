# DGCP™ Daily Public Signal

## 220: Integration Requirements

### Observation

Integration requirements continue defining the technical, informational, organizational, operational, and governance conditions needed for components, functions, services, and systems to establish and maintain functional connections.

These requirements can include compatible interfaces, standards, data structures, technical resources, defined responsibilities, information exchange, security arrangements, operational procedures, and coordination among participating systems.

### DGCP™ Insight

Integration requirements continue providing the conditions through which distinct components and systems can establish and maintain coordinated functional relationships.

---

## 221: Integration Capacity

### Observation

Integration capacity continues representing the available capability of systems and organizations to connect additional components, functions, information environments, services, or operational relationships within existing structures.

Integration capacity can depend on architecture, interfaces, standards, technical resources, information systems, workforce capability, governance arrangements, coordination mechanisms, and available operational capacity.

### DGCP™ Insight

Integration capacity continues reflecting the capabilities available to establish and maintain functional relationships among connected system components.

---

## 222: Integration Constraints

### Observation

Integration constraints continue occurring where technical, informational, organizational, operational, resource, or governance conditions limit the ability of systems or components to establish and maintain functional connections.

Constraints can arise from incompatible interfaces, standards, data structures, infrastructure, capacity limitations, resource availability, organizational boundaries, governance requirements, or dependencies across participating systems.

### DGCP™ Insight

Integration constraints continue defining conditions that limit how components, functions, and systems can be connected within coordinated operational structures.
