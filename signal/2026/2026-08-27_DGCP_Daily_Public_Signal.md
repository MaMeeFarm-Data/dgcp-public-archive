# DGCP™ Daily Public Signal

## 109 — System Integration

### Observation

System integration continues connecting different technologies, processes, data environments, organizations, and operational components so they can function within coordinated structures.

Integration depends on compatible interfaces, standards, information exchange, defined responsibilities, technical architecture, governance mechanisms, and operational alignment.

### DGCP™ Insight

System integration continues supporting coordinated system operation by connecting different components through compatible technical, informational, organizational, and governance structures.

---

## 110 — Interdependence

### Observation

Interdependence continues emerging where the operation of one system, organization, infrastructure, or process depends on capabilities, resources, information, or services provided by others.

These relationships extend across economic, technological, environmental, institutional, and social systems, creating networks of mutual operational dependency.

### DGCP™ Insight

Interdependence continues shaping system behavior through interconnected relationships among resources, capabilities, infrastructure, information, and operational functions.

---

## 111 — Coordination

### Observation

Coordination continues aligning activities, responsibilities, information, resources, and decisions across multiple participants and interconnected systems.

Effective coordination depends on defined roles, communication channels, shared information, operating procedures, decision mechanisms, and continuing interaction among participating entities.

### DGCP™ Insight

Coordination continues supporting collective system operation through structured alignment of activities, information, resources, responsibilities, and decisions.
