# DGCP™ Daily Public Signal

## 151: Redundancy

### Observation

Redundancy continues providing additional components, resources, connections, or capabilities that can support system functions when primary elements are unavailable, constrained, or operating under changed conditions.

Redundant structures can exist across infrastructure, technology, communications, supply networks, operational processes, and organizational systems, with their effectiveness depending on configuration, accessibility, and operational readiness.

### DGCP™ Insight

Redundancy continues supporting system continuity by providing additional capabilities and components that can perform or support required functions.

---

## 152: Alternative Pathways

### Observation

Alternative pathways continue providing different routes, connections, processes, suppliers, or mechanisms through which resources, information, services, or operational flows can move within interconnected systems.

Their availability depends on network structure, connectivity, capacity, compatibility, access conditions, and the ability of connected components to support redirected flows.

### DGCP™ Insight

Alternative pathways continue supporting system flexibility by providing additional routes through which functions and flows can be maintained or redirected.

---

## 153: Backup Capacity

### Observation

Backup capacity continues representing additional capability maintained or accessible beyond capacity used during normal operations to support particular functions when operating conditions change.

Backup capacity can exist across energy systems, computing infrastructure, transportation, communications, production, workforce, storage, and other operational environments.

### DGCP™ Insight

Backup capacity continues supporting operational continuity by providing additional capability that can become available when primary capacity is constrained or unavailable.
