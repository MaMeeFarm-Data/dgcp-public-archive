# DGCP™ Daily Public Signal

## 175: Diversification

### Observation

Diversification continues occurring where resources, capabilities, suppliers, services, technologies, activities, or operational functions are distributed across multiple sources, components, organizations, or pathways.

Diversification can emerge through system design, sourcing structures, investment patterns, infrastructure development, organizational arrangements, technological choices, and the availability of alternative resources or capabilities.

### DGCP™ Insight

Diversification continues shaping system structure by distributing resources, capabilities, functions, and dependencies across multiple sources and arrangements.

---

## 176: Distributed Capacity

### Observation

Distributed capacity continues occurring where production, processing, storage, computing, service, or other operational capabilities are located across multiple facilities, nodes, organizations, or system components.

Its structure can depend on infrastructure availability, technology, resource access, network connectivity, operational requirements, capital deployment, local capabilities, and relationships among distributed components.

### DGCP™ Insight

Distributed capacity continues reflecting how operational capability is positioned across multiple components, facilities, organizations, and network locations.

---

## 177: Geographic Distribution

### Observation

Geographic distribution continues describing how infrastructure, resources, capabilities, services, production, and operational activities are positioned across different physical locations and regions.

Distribution patterns can depend on natural resources, infrastructure access, transportation networks, energy availability, workforce, markets, environmental conditions, institutional arrangements, and historical development.

### DGCP™ Insight

Geographic distribution continues providing structural understanding of how system resources, capabilities, and activities are positioned across physical locations and regions.
