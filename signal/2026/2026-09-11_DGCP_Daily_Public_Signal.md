# DGCP™ Daily Public Signal

## 154: Reliability

### Observation

Reliability continues reflecting the ability of systems, infrastructure, components, and services to perform required functions consistently under defined operating conditions.

Reliability depends on system design, component condition, operational processes, maintenance, supporting resources, dependencies, monitoring, and the conditions under which functions are expected to operate.

### DGCP™ Insight

Reliability continues reflecting the consistency with which system functions and services can perform as required under defined operating conditions.

---

## 155: Availability

### Observation

Availability continues representing the extent to which systems, services, infrastructure, resources, or capabilities are accessible and ready for use when required.

Availability can depend on operational status, capacity, connectivity, resource access, maintenance conditions, supporting infrastructure, service dependencies, and mechanisms for restoring access.

### DGCP™ Insight

Availability continues reflecting whether required capabilities and services are accessible and operational when they are needed.

---

## 156: Service Continuity

### Observation

Service continuity continues describing the ability of systems and organizations to maintain or restore required services when operating conditions change or disruptions affect supporting components.

Continuity depends on interconnected capabilities including redundancy, alternative pathways, backup capacity, operational coordination, information, supporting resources, recovery processes, and infrastructure availability.

### DGCP™ Insight

Service continuity continues emerging from coordinated capabilities that support the maintenance or restoration of required services across changing operating conditions.
