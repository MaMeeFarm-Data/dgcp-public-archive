# DGCP™ Daily Public Signal

## 178: Access

### Observation

Access continues describing the ability of individuals, organizations, systems, or other participants to reach, obtain, use, or interact with resources, services, infrastructure, information, capabilities, or networks.

Access can depend on physical connectivity, technical compatibility, authorization, infrastructure availability, location, institutional arrangements, operational conditions, and other requirements governing participation or use.

### DGCP™ Insight

Access continues shaping system participation by determining how resources, services, infrastructure, information, and capabilities can be reached and used.

---

## 179: Access Control

### Observation

Access control continues providing mechanisms for determining who or what can enter, use, modify, operate, or interact with particular resources, systems, services, information, or infrastructure.

Access control can depend on identity, authorization, permissions, roles, policies, technical mechanisms, physical controls, institutional responsibilities, and documented governance arrangements.

### DGCP™ Insight

Access control continues structuring system interaction by defining and enforcing conditions under which resources, services, information, and capabilities can be used.

---

## 180: Resource Access

### Observation

Resource access continues describing the ability of participants, organizations, and systems to obtain or use materials, energy, infrastructure, information, capital, workforce capabilities, and other resources required for particular functions.

Resource access can depend on availability, location, infrastructure, distribution networks, authorization, market arrangements, institutional conditions, technical requirements, and relationships with resource providers.

### DGCP™ Insight

Resource access continues shaping operational capability through the conditions and pathways that connect system functions with required resources.
