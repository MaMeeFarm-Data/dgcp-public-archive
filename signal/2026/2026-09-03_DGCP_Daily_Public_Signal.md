# DGCP™ Daily Public Signal

## 130: Dependencies

### Observation

Dependencies continue forming where components, systems, organizations, processes, or infrastructures rely on resources, information, services, capabilities, or functions provided by others.

Dependencies can exist across technical, physical, operational, organizational, economic, and institutional structures and can extend across multiple system boundaries.

### DGCP™ Insight

Dependencies continue shaping system behavior by linking the operation of individual components to capabilities, resources, services, and functions provided elsewhere.

---

## 131: Critical Dependencies

### Observation

Critical dependencies continue representing relationships in which essential functions rely on particular resources, services, infrastructure, information, capabilities, or supporting systems.

Their significance depends on operational context, the function being supported, available alternatives, system configuration, and the consequences associated with interruption or loss of support.

### DGCP™ Insight

Critical dependencies continue identifying relationships where the continuity of essential functions is closely connected to the availability of supporting capabilities and resources.

---

## 132: Dependency Mapping

### Observation

Dependency mapping continues providing structured methods for identifying and documenting relationships among components, functions, resources, services, infrastructure, organizations, and supporting systems.

Mapping can reveal direct and indirect dependencies, interconnected pathways, shared supporting resources, and relationships that extend across multiple system layers and boundaries.

### DGCP™ Insight

Dependency mapping continues supporting structural understanding by making interconnected dependency relationships visible and traceable across complex systems.
