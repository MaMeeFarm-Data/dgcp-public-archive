# DGCP™ Daily Public Signal

## 214: System Interaction

### Observation

System interaction continues occurring where distinct systems exchange information, resources, services, commands, outputs, or other operational inputs through defined relationships and interfaces.

These interactions can extend across physical infrastructure, digital networks, organizations, institutions, supply systems, and services while participating systems retain their own functions, responsibilities, and operational structures.

### DGCP™ Insight

System interaction continues connecting distinct systems through exchanges of information, resources, services, functions, and operational outputs.

---

## 215: Cross-System Coordination

### Observation

Cross-system coordination continues aligning activities, information, resources, responsibilities, and decisions among distinct systems participating in shared or interconnected functions.

Coordination can depend on defined roles, communication channels, information exchange, compatible procedures, decision arrangements, shared standards, interfaces, and mechanisms for managing activities across system boundaries.

### DGCP™ Insight

Cross-system coordination continues supporting interconnected operation by aligning activities, information, resources, responsibilities, and decisions across distinct systems.

---

## 216: Coordinated System Operation

### Observation

Coordinated system operation continues occurring where distinct systems align their activities, information flows, resources, responsibilities, and operational timing in support of interconnected functions.

Such operation can depend on defined interfaces, compatible procedures, shared standards, communication channels, decision arrangements, operational visibility, and continuing coordination across participating systems.

### DGCP™ Insight

Coordinated system operation continues connecting system interaction with structured alignment across activities, information, resources, responsibilities, and operational functions.
