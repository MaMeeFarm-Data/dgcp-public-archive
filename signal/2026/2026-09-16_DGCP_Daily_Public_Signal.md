# DGCP™ Daily Public Signal

## 169: System Exposure

### Observation

System exposure continues describing the extent to which systems, infrastructure, organizations, resources, or functions are positioned in relation to conditions, events, dependencies, or external environments that can affect their operation.

Exposure can vary according to location, network position, dependency structure, resource requirements, infrastructure connections, operating conditions, and the duration or intensity of contact with relevant conditions.

### DGCP™ Insight

System exposure continues providing structural understanding of how system components and functions are positioned in relation to conditions that can affect their operation.

---

## 170: Vulnerability

### Observation

Vulnerability continues reflecting characteristics or conditions that can influence how strongly a system, component, organization, or function is affected when exposed to disruption or changing operating conditions.

Vulnerability can depend on dependencies, available capacity, resource access, redundancy, alternative pathways, system configuration, operational flexibility, preparedness, and recovery capabilities.

### DGCP™ Insight

Vulnerability continues reflecting how system characteristics and available capabilities shape the effects that exposure can produce within interconnected systems.

---

## 171: Systemic Risk

### Observation

Systemic risk continues arising where disruption or loss of function within interconnected systems can extend beyond individual components through dependencies, networks, shared infrastructure, concentrated functions, or common supporting resources.

Its structure depends on the relationships among exposure, vulnerability, connectivity, concentration, propagation pathways, available alternatives, and the functions supported across the wider system.

### DGCP™ Insight

Systemic risk continues reflecting the possibility that disruption can extend through interconnected relationships and affect functions beyond its initial point of origin.
