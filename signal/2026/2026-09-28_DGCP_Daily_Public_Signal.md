# DGCP™ Daily Public Signal

## 205: System Maturity

### Observation

System maturity continues reflecting the development of structures, processes, responsibilities, controls, information practices, and operational capabilities that support consistent system functioning over time.

Maturity can depend on documented processes, defined roles, accumulated experience, institutional learning, monitoring, feedback, verification, corrective processes, governance arrangements, and the continuing maintenance of system capabilities.

### DGCP™ Insight

System maturity continues reflecting the development and integration of structures, processes, knowledge, controls, and capabilities that support consistent system operation.

---

## 206: Institutional Capacity

### Observation

Institutional capacity continues representing the ability of institutions and organizations to perform defined responsibilities, maintain required functions, coordinate activities, manage resources, and implement established processes.

Institutional capacity can depend on authority, decision rights, workforce, knowledge, financial and technical resources, information systems, governance arrangements, organizational structures, coordination mechanisms, and institutional continuity.

### DGCP™ Insight

Institutional capacity continues reflecting the combined organizational, informational, resource, governance, and coordination capabilities available to perform defined institutional functions.

---

## 207: Operational Capability

### Observation

Operational capability continues representing the ability of systems and organizations to perform required activities and functions through available people, processes, technology, infrastructure, information, resources, and supporting services.

Operational capability can depend on resource availability, workforce capacity, infrastructure access, technical systems, service dependencies, procedures, coordination, information flows, and the conditions under which operations are performed.

### DGCP™ Insight

Operational capability continues reflecting the combined resources, processes, infrastructure, information, and supporting capabilities available to perform required system functions.
