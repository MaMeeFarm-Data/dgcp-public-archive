## 91 — Global Cooperation

### Observation

Global cooperation continues connecting governments, institutions, organizations, businesses, and communities across shared economic, social, environmental, technical, and security matters.

### Observation

International organizations continue providing institutional structures for cooperation, coordination, information exchange, standard setting, technical activity, and collective engagement across participating members.

Their activities depend on defined mandates, governance structures, member participation, funding mechanisms, professional capabilities, information systems, and relationships with national and international institutions.

### Observation
