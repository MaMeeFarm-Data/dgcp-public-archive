# DGCP™ Daily Public Signal

## 139: Operational Dependencies

### Observation

Operational dependencies continue forming where processes, functions, teams, systems, or organizations rely on other activities, capabilities, information, resources, or services to maintain ongoing operations.

These dependencies can extend across internal and external boundaries and may involve sequencing, timing, access, coordination, and the availability of supporting functions.

### DGCP™ Insight

Operational dependencies continue shaping how system functions rely on interconnected activities, capabilities, information, and resources during ongoing operations.

---

## 140: Service Dependencies

### Observation

Service dependencies continue forming where organizations, users, processes, or systems rely on services provided by internal or external entities to perform required functions.

These services can include digital platforms, communications, utilities, logistics, financial services, technical support, information services, and other operational capabilities.

### DGCP™ Insight

Service dependencies continue connecting system functions to the external and internal services required to support ongoing activity.

---

## 141: Infrastructure Dependencies

### Observation

Infrastructure dependencies continue forming where system functions rely on physical or digital infrastructure such as energy networks, transportation systems, telecommunications, computing facilities, water systems, and other supporting assets.

These dependencies can extend across sectors and geographic areas through interconnected networks, shared facilities, and supporting service structures.

### DGCP™ Insight

Infrastructure dependencies continue shaping system capability through reliance on interconnected physical and digital infrastructure.
