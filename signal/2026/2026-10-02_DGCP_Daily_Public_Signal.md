# DGCP™ Daily Public Signal

## 217: System Alignment

### Observation

System alignment continues occurring where structures, functions, responsibilities, resources, information, and operational arrangements across interconnected systems are organized to support compatible or shared requirements.

Alignment can depend on defined objectives, system boundaries, interfaces, standards, governance arrangements, information exchange, responsibilities, and coordination across participating systems.

### DGCP™ Insight

System alignment continues supporting interconnected operation by organizing system structures, functions, responsibilities, and resources around compatible requirements.

---

## 218: Functional Alignment

### Observation

Functional alignment continues occurring where related functions across systems, organizations, or operational components are organized to support compatible roles, requirements, outputs, and interactions.

Functional alignment can depend on clear responsibilities, defined processes, interfaces, information exchange, compatible procedures, resource relationships, and understanding of how individual functions contribute to broader system activity.

### DGCP™ Insight

Functional alignment continues connecting related functions through compatible roles, processes, responsibilities, outputs, and operational relationships.

---

## 219: Operational Alignment

### Observation

Operational alignment continues occurring where activities, processes, resources, information, responsibilities, and timing are coordinated across interconnected operational environments.

Alignment can depend on communication, shared information, defined procedures, decision arrangements, resource coordination, compatible operating practices, and mechanisms for maintaining coordination across system boundaries.

### DGCP™ Insight

Operational alignment continues supporting coordinated activity by connecting processes, resources, information, responsibilities, and timing across operational systems.
