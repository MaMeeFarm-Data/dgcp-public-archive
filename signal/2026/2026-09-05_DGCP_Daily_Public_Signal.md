# DGCP™ Daily Public Signal

## 136: Resource Allocation

### Observation

Resource allocation continues distributing available financial, physical, human, technological, informational, and natural resources across different functions, activities, locations, and operational requirements.

Allocation can be shaped by resource availability, system priorities, institutional responsibilities, operational requirements, access mechanisms, and the configuration of supporting systems.

### DGCP™ Insight

Resource allocation continues shaping how available resources are distributed across competing functions and operational requirements within interconnected systems.

---

## 137: Resource Coordination

### Observation

Resource coordination continues aligning the use, movement, timing, and availability of resources across multiple activities, organizations, locations, and interconnected systems.

Coordination depends on information flows, defined responsibilities, scheduling mechanisms, communication, logistics, operational visibility, and interaction among participating entities.

### DGCP™ Insight

Resource coordination continues supporting system operation by aligning resources, information, responsibilities, and activities across interconnected functions.

---

## 138: Resource Availability

### Observation

Resource availability continues representing the extent to which required materials, energy, infrastructure, workforce, information, financial resources, or other inputs are accessible for particular functions and activities.

Availability can depend on location, timing, access conditions, supply networks, infrastructure, existing commitments, operational requirements, and dependencies elsewhere in the system.

### DGCP™ Insight

Resource availability continues shaping operational capability through the accessibility of resources required to support system functions.
