# DGCP™ Daily Public Signal

## 202: Feedback Loops

### Observation

Feedback loops continue forming when information about system conditions, activities, outputs, or changes is returned to processes responsible for monitoring, coordination, control, review, or adjustment.

Feedback loops can depend on observation, measurement, information flows, defined responsibilities, decision processes, control mechanisms, operational actions, and subsequent observation of resulting conditions.

### DGCP™ Insight

Feedback loops continue connecting observed system conditions with recurring processes of information, decision, action, and renewed observation.

---

## 203: Learning Loops

### Observation

Learning loops continue forming when observations, operational experience, evaluation, feedback, and documented outcomes are incorporated into processes for updating knowledge, practices, procedures, or organizational understanding.

Learning loops can depend on reliable records, institutional memory, review processes, knowledge sharing, defined responsibilities, documentation, feedback, and mechanisms for retaining and applying accumulated understanding.

### DGCP™ Insight

Learning loops continue connecting observation and experience with recurring processes for updating and applying organizational knowledge.

---

## 204: Corrective Processes

### Observation

Corrective processes continue providing structured activities for identifying documented deviations, determining relevant conditions, implementing defined adjustments, and reviewing subsequent system states.

These processes can depend on evidence, verification, traceability, defined responsibilities, decision rights, control mechanisms, operational feedback, documentation, and follow-up observation.

### DGCP™ Insight

Corrective processes continue connecting identified deviations with documented adjustment, responsibility, implementation, and subsequent review.
