# DGCP™ Daily Public Signal

## 115 — Performance

### Observation

Performance continues reflecting how systems, organizations, processes, and operational activities function in relation to defined objectives, responsibilities, resources, and expected outputs.

Understanding performance depends on reliable information, appropriate measures, operational context, documented processes, and consistent observation across relevant periods.

### DGCP™ Insight

## 116 — Evaluation

### Observation

Evaluation continues providing structured processes for examining activities, programs, systems, and outcomes against defined objectives, criteria, evidence, and observed results.

Evaluation depends on reliable data, appropriate methods, documented criteria, contextual understanding, analytical processes, and mechanisms for reviewing findings.

### DGCP™ Insight

## 117 — Accountability

### Observation

Accountability continues establishing relationships between responsibilities, decisions, actions, documentation, oversight, and resulting outcomes across organizations and institutional systems.

Accountability mechanisms depend on clearly defined roles, accessible information, traceable records, reporting structures, review processes, and appropriate oversight.

### DGCP™ Insight

Accountability continues supporting institutional clarity by connecting defined responsibilities and decisions with documented actions, outcomes, and review mechanisms.
