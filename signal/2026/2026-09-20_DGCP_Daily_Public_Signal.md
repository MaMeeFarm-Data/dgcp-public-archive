# DGCP™ Daily Public Signal

## 181: Authority

### Observation

Authority continues defining the recognized ability of individuals, organizations, institutions, or designated roles to direct actions, establish requirements, approve activities, allocate responsibilities, or exercise control within defined areas of a system.

Authority can be established through laws, regulations, governance arrangements, organizational structures, contracts, policies, mandates, delegated powers, and other documented institutional mechanisms.

### DGCP™ Insight

Authority continues structuring system governance by defining where recognized powers and responsibilities are located within organizational and institutional arrangements.

---

## 182: Decision Rights

### Observation

Decision rights continue defining which individuals, roles, organizations, or institutions are authorized to make particular decisions within defined operational and governance contexts.

Decision rights can specify authority over resources, processes, information, approvals, priorities, access, operational changes, and other activities requiring defined responsibility for decision making.

### DGCP™ Insight

Decision rights continue providing structural clarity by identifying where authority to make particular decisions is assigned within systems and organizations.

---

## 183: Operational Responsibility

### Observation

Operational responsibility continues assigning individuals, teams, organizations, or institutional roles responsibility for performing, maintaining, coordinating, monitoring, or supporting particular system functions and activities.

Operational responsibilities can depend on organizational structures, defined roles, procedures, service arrangements, governance requirements, contractual obligations, and relationships among participating entities.

### DGCP™ Insight

Operational responsibility continues connecting defined roles with the activities and functions they are expected to perform or support within operational systems.
