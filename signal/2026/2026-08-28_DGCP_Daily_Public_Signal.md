# DGCP™ Daily Public Signal

## 112 — Adaptation

### Observation

Adaptation continues occurring as organizations and systems adjust processes, structures, resources, and operational practices in response to changing conditions and observed constraints.

Adaptive capacity depends on information, feedback mechanisms, available resources, institutional flexibility, operational capabilities, and the ability to translate observed conditions into practical adjustments.

### DGCP™ Insight

## 113 — Organizational Learning

### Observation

Organizational learning continues developing through experience, observation, evaluation, documentation, and the incorporation of knowledge into organizational processes and practices.

Learning depends on reliable information, institutional memory, feedback mechanisms, knowledge management, communication, review processes, and opportunities to apply accumulated understanding.

### DGCP™ Insight

Organizational learning continues strengthening institutional capability by connecting experience, evidence, documented knowledge, and operational practice.

## 114 — Knowledge Sharing

### Observation

Knowledge sharing continues enabling information, experience, methods, and documented understanding to move across individuals, teams, organizations, and institutional networks.
