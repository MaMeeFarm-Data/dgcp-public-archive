# DGCP™ Daily Public Signal

## 133: Capacity

### Observation

Capacity continues representing the available capability of systems, infrastructure, organizations, processes, or resources to perform functions, provide services, process flows, or support operational activity.

Capacity can depend on physical assets, technology, workforce, energy, information, resources, operating conditions, and the configuration of supporting systems.

### DGCP™ Insight

Capacity continues defining the level of functional capability available within systems under particular operational conditions.

---

## 134: Capacity Allocation

### Observation

Capacity allocation continues distributing available resources, infrastructure, processing capability, services, or operational capability across competing functions, activities, users, or requirements.

Allocation can be shaped by operational priorities, resource availability, system configuration, contractual arrangements, institutional responsibilities, and mechanisms for coordinating access.

### DGCP™ Insight

Capacity allocation continues shaping how available system capability is distributed across different functions, activities, and operational requirements.

---

## 135: Capacity Constraints

### Observation

Capacity constraints continue occurring where available capability limits the volume, scale, speed, or range of activities that systems, infrastructure, organizations, or processes can support.

Constraints can emerge from physical infrastructure, resource availability, workforce, technology, energy, connectivity, processing capability, operational configuration, or dependencies elsewhere in the system.

### DGCP™ Insight

Capacity constraints continue defining operational limits where available capabilities and supporting resources restrict the scale or range of system activity.
