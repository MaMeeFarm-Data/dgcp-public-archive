# DGCP™ Daily Public Signal

## 127: System Boundaries

### Observation

System boundaries continue defining the scope within which components, functions, resources, responsibilities, and interactions are considered part of a system.

Boundaries can separate systems from their surrounding environments while remaining connected through exchanges of information, resources, services, authority, and other operational inputs and outputs.

### DGCP™ Insight

System boundaries continue providing structural clarity by distinguishing what is included within a system while identifying where interaction with external environments occurs.

---

## 128: Interfaces

### Observation

Interfaces continue providing defined structures through which components, systems, organizations, technologies, and processes interact across boundaries.

Interfaces can support exchanges of information, resources, services, commands, responsibilities, and operational outputs through technical, physical, organizational, or procedural arrangements.

### DGCP™ Insight

Interfaces continue supporting structured interaction by defining how separate components and systems exchange information, resources, services, and operational functions.

---

## 129: Connection Points

### Observation

Connection points continue representing locations or mechanisms where components, networks, processes, infrastructures, or organizations establish direct operational relationships.

These points can concentrate exchanges of information, resources, services, traffic, authority, or other system flows and can link otherwise separate parts of a larger structure.

### DGCP™ Insight

Connection points continue linking system structures by providing identifiable locations and mechanisms through which interactions and flows occur.
