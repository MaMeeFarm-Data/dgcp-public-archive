# DGCP™ Daily Public Signal

## 76 — Governance

### Observation

Governance continues providing structures through which authority, responsibilities, rules, decisions, and accountability are organized across public, private, and institutional systems.

Governance processes depend on defined roles, decision mechanisms, policies, information flows, oversight structures, and mechanisms for implementation and review.

## 77 — Public Administration

### Observation

Public administration continues supporting the implementation of public policies, programs, services, and institutional responsibilities across different levels of government.

Administrative operations depend on interconnected public institutions, personnel, procedures, information systems, budgets, regulatory frameworks, and service-delivery mechanisms.

## 78 — Institutions

### Observation

Institutions continue organizing recurring social, economic, legal, and administrative activities through established rules, roles, procedures, and organizational structures.
