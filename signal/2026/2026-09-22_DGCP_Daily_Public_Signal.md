# DGCP™ Daily Public Signal

## 187: Verification

### Observation

Verification continues providing structured processes for checking whether information, records, components, processes, outputs, or system activities conform to defined requirements, specifications, or documented conditions.

Verification can depend on evidence, measurements, records, reference criteria, inspection, testing, documentation, traceability, and repeatable procedures for comparing observed states with established requirements.

### DGCP™ Insight

Verification continues supporting structured system understanding by connecting observable evidence with defined requirements and documented conditions.

---

## 188: Validation

### Observation

Validation continues providing structured processes for determining whether systems, processes, methods, outputs, or information are suitable for their defined purpose or intended use within a particular context.

Validation can depend on defined objectives, relevant evidence, documented criteria, testing, observation, contextual requirements, review processes, and records supporting the resulting determination.

### DGCP™ Insight

Validation continues connecting evidence and defined purpose by examining whether systems, processes, methods, or outputs are suitable for their intended use.

---

## 189: Assurance

### Observation

Assurance continues providing structured confidence that defined requirements, responsibilities, processes, controls, or system functions are being addressed through appropriate evidence, verification, validation, review, and oversight arrangements.

Assurance can depend on reliable records, traceability, defined criteria, independent or designated review, documented controls, monitoring, verification activities, and mechanisms for identifying and addressing deviations.

### DGCP™ Insight

Assurance continues supporting confidence in system operation through structured evidence, controls, review, verification, validation, and oversight.
