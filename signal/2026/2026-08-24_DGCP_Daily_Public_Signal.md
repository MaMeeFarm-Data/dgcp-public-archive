## 100 — Digital Transformation

### Observation

Digital transformation continues integrating digital technologies, data, software, and connected systems into organizational processes, services, operations, and decision environments.

Transformation activities depend on existing infrastructure, data capabilities, workforce skills, governance, cybersecurity, organizational processes, investment, and mechanisms for implementing technological change.

## 101 — Smart Infrastructure

### Observation

Smart infrastructure continues combining physical assets with sensors, communication networks, data systems, computing capabilities, and digital controls to support monitoring and operational management.

These systems depend on interconnected physical infrastructure, reliable connectivity, data collection, software, cybersecurity, technical standards, and mechanisms for human oversight.

### DGCP™ Insight

Smart infrastructure continues connecting physical and digital capabilities through coordinated sensing, communication, data processing, and operational control.
