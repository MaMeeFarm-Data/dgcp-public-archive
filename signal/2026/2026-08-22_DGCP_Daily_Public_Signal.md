# DGCP™ Daily Public Signal

## 94 — Economic Resilience

### Observation

Economic resilience reflects the capacity of economic systems to continue functioning, absorb disruption, adjust to changing conditions, and restore activity across interconnected sectors.

This capacity depends on diverse production systems, financial structures, infrastructure, supply networks, institutions, resources, information, and mechanisms for coordination and adaptation.

### DGCP™ Insight

Economic resilience remains structurally connected to the capacity of interconnected economic systems to absorb disruption, adapt operations, and maintain or restore essential functions.

---

## 95 — Business Continuity

### Observation

Business continuity continues supporting the maintenance and recovery of essential organizational functions when normal operations are disrupted.

Continuity capabilities depend on defined responsibilities, operational procedures, workforce availability, technology, data, facilities, supply networks, communication systems, and recovery arrangements.

### DGCP™ Insight

Business continuity continues supporting organizational resilience through structured preparation, operational coordination, and recovery capabilities.

---

## 96 — Risk Management

### Observation

Risk management continues providing structured processes for identifying, assessing, monitoring, and responding to uncertainties and exposures across organizational and operational environments.

Risk management depends on reliable information, defined responsibilities, assessment methods, controls, monitoring mechanisms, decision processes, and continuing review.

### DGCP™ Insight

Risk management continues supporting informed decision-making through structured identification, assessment, monitoring, and treatment of risk.
