# DGCP™ Daily Public Signal

## 148: Bottlenecks

### Observation

Bottlenecks continue forming where the capacity of a particular component, process, pathway, or resource is lower than the demand or flow placed upon it within a system.

Bottlenecks can occur across physical infrastructure, production processes, logistics networks, digital systems, organizational workflows, and service environments, depending on system configuration and operating conditions.

### DGCP™ Insight

Bottlenecks continue shaping system flows where limited capacity within particular components or pathways constrains the movement or processing of activity.

---

## 149: Chokepoints

### Observation

Chokepoints continue representing concentrated locations, routes, facilities, interfaces, or system components through which significant flows or activities must pass within particular network structures.

Their structural importance can depend on network configuration, available alternative pathways, connected functions, flow concentration, and the systems relying on continued access through those points.

### DGCP™ Insight

Chokepoints continue concentrating system flows through identifiable points or pathways whose structural significance depends on their role within interconnected networks.

---

## 150: Constraint Management

### Observation

Constraint management continues organizing processes for identifying, monitoring, coordinating, and addressing limitations that affect system capacity, flows, resources, or operational functions.

These processes can depend on reliable observation, defined responsibilities, resource coordination, capacity information, operational adjustments, and continuing review of system conditions.

### DGCP™ Insight

Constraint management continues supporting structured system operation by connecting observed limitations with coordinated monitoring, resource use, operational adjustment, and review.
