# DGCP™ Daily Public Signal

## 184: Control Systems

### Observation

Control systems continue providing structured arrangements for monitoring conditions, comparing observed states with defined requirements, and directing adjustments within technical, operational, organizational, and institutional systems.

Control systems can depend on information, measurement, feedback, defined responsibilities, decision processes, operating rules, technical components, and mechanisms for implementing adjustments.

### DGCP™ Insight

Control systems continue supporting structured system operation by connecting observation, information, decisions, feedback, and adjustment mechanisms.

---

## 185: Control Points

### Observation

Control points continue representing identifiable locations, interfaces, processes, or decision stages where system activities, flows, access, resources, or operations can be monitored, directed, authorized, restricted, or adjusted.

Control points can exist across physical infrastructure, digital systems, organizational processes, supply networks, institutional arrangements, and operational workflows.

### DGCP™ Insight

Control points continue providing identifiable positions within system structures where defined forms of monitoring, authorization, direction, or adjustment can occur.

---

## 186: Control Mechanisms

### Observation

Control mechanisms continue providing the specific technical, procedural, organizational, or institutional means through which defined requirements, decisions, limits, permissions, or adjustments are implemented within systems.

These mechanisms can include technical controls, operating procedures, authorization processes, feedback structures, rules, standards, verification processes, and other arrangements that influence system operation.

### DGCP™ Insight

Control mechanisms continue translating defined requirements and decisions into operational constraints, permissions, adjustments, and system actions.
