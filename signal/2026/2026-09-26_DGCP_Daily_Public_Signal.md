# DGCP™ Daily Public Signal

## 199: System Monitoring

### Observation

System monitoring continues providing structured processes for observing the condition, activity, performance, and operational state of components, services, infrastructure, and interconnected systems over time.

Monitoring can depend on sensors, measurements, data collection, reporting mechanisms, operational records, defined indicators, information systems, and procedures for maintaining continuing visibility into relevant system conditions.

### DGCP™ Insight

System monitoring continues supporting operational visibility by connecting observed conditions, measurements, records, and indicators across system activities.

---

## 200: Continuous Observation

### Observation

Continuous observation continues maintaining ongoing or repeated collection of information about system conditions, activities, relationships, and changes across relevant periods.

Continuous observation can depend on persistent data sources, measurement systems, reporting processes, documentation, timestamps, information continuity, and consistent methods for preserving observations over time.

### DGCP™ Insight

Continuous observation continues supporting longitudinal system understanding by preserving structured observations across changing conditions and time periods.

---

## 201: Operational Feedback

### Observation

Operational feedback continues returning information about system conditions, activities, outputs, and changes into processes responsible for monitoring, coordination, control, review, or adjustment.

Feedback can depend on reliable observations, timely information flows, defined communication pathways, decision structures, operational responsibilities, and mechanisms through which information can inform subsequent system activity.

### DGCP™ Insight

Operational feedback continues connecting observed system conditions with processes for coordination, control, review, and operational adjustment.
