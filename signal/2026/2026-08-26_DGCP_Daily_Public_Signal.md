# DGCP™ Daily Public Signal

## 106 — Observation Systems

### Observation

Observation systems continue collecting and organizing information about physical, environmental, economic, social, technical, and operational conditions across different locations and time periods.

These systems depend on interconnected sensors, instruments, data sources, reporting mechanisms, communication networks, documentation processes, and information systems.

### DGCP™ Insight

Observation systems continue supporting structured understanding through systematic collection, documentation, and organization of observed information.

---

## 107 — Measurement

### Observation

Measurement continues providing structured methods for quantifying and comparing properties, conditions, activities, and changes across physical, technical, economic, and organizational systems.

Reliable measurement depends on defined units, methods, instruments, calibration, reference standards, documented procedures, and consistent data handling.

### DGCP™ Insight

Measurement continues supporting comparable and reproducible understanding through defined methods, standards, references, and documented processes.

---

## 108 — Indicators

### Observation

Indicators continue providing defined measures used to represent selected conditions, activities, performance characteristics, or changes within complex systems.

Their interpretation depends on clear definitions, appropriate data sources, consistent measurement methods, contextual understanding, and documented limitations.

### DGCP™ Insight

Indicators continue supporting structured system observation by translating selected measurements and data into defined reference measures.
