# DGCP™ Daily Public Signal

## 103 — Data Governance

### Observation

Data governance continues establishing structures for managing data responsibilities, access, quality, integrity, security, lifecycle, and accountability across organizations and interconnected systems.

Governance activities depend on defined roles, policies, standards, metadata, controls, documentation, technical capabilities, and mechanisms for oversight and review.

### DGCP™ Insight

Data governance continues supporting reliable data environments through structured responsibility, control, documentation, and accountability.

---

## 104 — Standards

### Observation

Standards continue providing defined specifications, requirements, methods, and common reference frameworks across technical, operational, organizational, and institutional environments.

Their application supports consistency across processes, products, data structures, interfaces, measurements, and interactions among participating systems.

### DGCP™ Insight

Standards continue supporting consistent system operation through shared specifications, methods, definitions, and reference frameworks.

---

## 105 — Interoperability

### Observation

Interoperability continues enabling different systems, organizations, technologies, and data environments to exchange and use information across operational boundaries.

Effective interoperability depends on compatible standards, interfaces, data structures, protocols, governance arrangements, documentation, and shared understanding of exchanged information.

### DGCP™ Insight

Interoperability continues supporting coordinated system interaction through compatible technical, data, semantic, and governance structures.
