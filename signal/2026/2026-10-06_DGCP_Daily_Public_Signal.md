# DGCP™ Daily Public Signal

## 229: Coupling

### Observation

Coupling continues describing the degree of connection and dependency between components, functions, processes, organizations, or systems whose activities or operating conditions are linked through defined relationships.

Coupling can arise through information exchange, resource flows, technical interfaces, shared infrastructure, service dependencies, operational sequencing, timing requirements, control relationships, or other mechanisms connecting system functions.

### DGCP™ Insight

Coupling continues reflecting the degree to which components and systems are connected through dependencies, interactions, interfaces, and operational relationships.

---

## 230: Tight Coupling

### Observation

Tight coupling continues occurring where components, functions, processes, or systems maintain closely connected relationships with limited separation in timing, sequencing, interfaces, resources, or operational dependencies.

Changes or interruptions within tightly coupled relationships can require corresponding responses elsewhere because participating functions depend closely on specific inputs, outputs, timing, conditions, or connected operations.

### DGCP™ Insight

Tight coupling continues reflecting closely connected system relationships in which functions and operations depend strongly on specific interactions, sequences, timing, or supporting conditions.

---

## 231: Loose Coupling

### Observation

Loose coupling continues occurring where components, functions, processes, or systems remain connected while retaining greater operational separation, flexibility, or independence within their relationships.

Loosely coupled structures can allow participating components to interact through defined interfaces, information exchanges, services, or resource relationships without requiring continuous synchronization or direct dependence across every operational activity.

### DGCP™ Insight

Loose coupling continues reflecting system relationships that maintain connection while preserving greater separation and operational independence among participating components.
