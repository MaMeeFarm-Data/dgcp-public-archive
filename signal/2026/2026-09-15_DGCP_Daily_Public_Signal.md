# DGCP™ Daily Public Signal

## 166: Preparedness

### Observation

Preparedness continues establishing arrangements, resources, procedures, responsibilities, and capabilities that can support system operation and organized response when disruptions or changing conditions occur.

Preparedness can depend on planning, training, information, communication, resource availability, defined roles, operational procedures, coordination mechanisms, and the maintenance of supporting capabilities.

### DGCP™ Insight

Preparedness continues supporting system readiness through established capabilities, resources, responsibilities, procedures, and coordination arrangements.

---

## 167: Response Capacity

### Observation

Response capacity continues representing the available capability of systems and organizations to mobilize resources, personnel, information, infrastructure, and operational functions when conditions require an organized response.

Response capacity can depend on resource availability, workforce capability, communication, logistics, decision structures, infrastructure access, service availability, coordination, and the ability to activate supporting arrangements.

### DGCP™ Insight

Response capacity continues reflecting the capabilities and resources available to support organized action under changing or disrupted operating conditions.

---

## 168: Recovery Coordination

### Observation

Recovery coordination continues aligning responsibilities, information, resources, priorities, activities, and supporting capabilities across participants involved in restoring affected functions and services.

Coordination can extend across organizations, infrastructure operators, service providers, technical functions, institutions, and other connected entities through defined communication, information-sharing, decision, and operational arrangements.

### DGCP™ Insight

Recovery coordination continues supporting restoration by aligning participants, information, resources, responsibilities, and activities across interconnected recovery functions.
