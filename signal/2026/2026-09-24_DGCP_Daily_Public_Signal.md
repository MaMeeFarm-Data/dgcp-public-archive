# DGCP™ Daily Public Signal

## 193: Transparency

### Observation

Transparency continues providing structured access to relevant information about activities, processes, decisions, responsibilities, records, and system conditions within defined contexts.

Transparency can depend on documentation, information availability, disclosure arrangements, clear responsibilities, accessible records, reporting structures, and mechanisms that allow relevant information to be understood and examined.

### DGCP™ Insight

Transparency continues supporting system understanding by making relevant information about processes, decisions, responsibilities, and activities accessible within defined contexts.

---

## 194: Visibility

### Observation

Visibility continues describing the extent to which system conditions, activities, flows, dependencies, resources, processes, and changes can be observed through available information and monitoring structures.

Visibility can depend on data availability, observation systems, reporting mechanisms, monitoring capabilities, documentation, information sharing, and connections among relevant system components.

### DGCP™ Insight

Visibility continues supporting system observation by making relevant conditions, activities, relationships, and changes observable across interconnected structures.

---

## 195: Auditability

### Observation

Auditability continues reflecting the extent to which records, activities, decisions, controls, processes, and outputs can be systematically examined against defined criteria and documented evidence.

Auditability can depend on traceable records, provenance, timestamps, documentation, defined responsibilities, preserved evidence, consistent procedures, and access to information required for review.

### DGCP™ Insight

Auditability continues supporting structured examination by preserving the evidence, records, relationships, and documentation required to review system activities and outputs.
