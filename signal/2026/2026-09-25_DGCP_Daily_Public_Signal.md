# DGCP™ Daily Public Signal

## 196: Data Quality

### Observation

Data quality continues reflecting the extent to which data is suitable for defined uses within particular operational, analytical, administrative, or decision contexts.

Data quality can depend on characteristics such as accuracy, completeness, timeliness, consistency, relevance, validity, documentation, and the conditions under which data is collected, maintained, and used.

### DGCP™ Insight

Data quality continues supporting reliable system understanding by connecting data characteristics with defined purposes, contexts, and requirements.

---

## 197: Data Consistency

### Observation

Data consistency continues describing the extent to which data values, definitions, formats, classifications, relationships, and records remain coherent across datasets, systems, processes, and time periods.

Consistency can depend on shared definitions, standards, data structures, identifiers, validation rules, version controls, documentation, and coordinated methods for creating and maintaining records.

### DGCP™ Insight

Data consistency continues supporting coherent information environments by maintaining compatible definitions, structures, relationships, and records across interconnected systems.

---

## 198: Data Reliability

### Observation

Data reliability continues reflecting the extent to which data can consistently support its defined use when produced, maintained, and interpreted under documented conditions.

Reliability can depend on source integrity, collection methods, measurement processes, data quality controls, consistency, provenance, validation, documentation, and the stability of processes used to generate and maintain data.

### DGCP™ Insight

Data reliability continues supporting dependable information use through consistent data processes, documented sources, quality controls, and preserved context.
