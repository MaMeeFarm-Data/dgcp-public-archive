# DGCP™ Daily Public Signal

## 226: Shared Dependencies

### Observation

Shared dependencies continue forming where multiple systems, organizations, services, or operational functions rely on the same infrastructure, resources, services, information, technologies, or supporting capabilities.

These dependencies can connect otherwise distinct systems through common supporting elements whose availability, capacity, accessibility, or operational condition influences multiple participating functions.

### DGCP™ Insight

Shared dependencies continue connecting distinct systems through reliance on common infrastructure, resources, services, information, and supporting capabilities.

---

## 227: Common Dependencies

### Observation

Common dependencies continue occurring where similar dependency relationships exist across multiple systems, organizations, processes, or operational environments.

These relationships can involve reliance on comparable categories of infrastructure, resources, technologies, services, information, workforce capabilities, or institutional arrangements even when the specific supporting components are not identical.

### DGCP™ Insight

Common dependencies continue revealing recurring patterns of reliance across otherwise distinct systems, functions, and operational environments.

---

## 228: Dependency Overlap

### Observation

Dependency overlap continues occurring where dependency relationships across different systems intersect through shared providers, infrastructure, resources, services, technologies, locations, networks, or supporting functions.

Overlap can emerge directly through reliance on the same supporting component or indirectly through interconnected dependency chains that converge on common capabilities elsewhere within the wider system structure.

### DGCP™ Insight

Dependency overlap continues revealing where otherwise distinct dependency relationships intersect through common supporting components, capabilities, and interconnected pathways.
