# DGCP™ Daily Public Signal

## 121 — Civilization Systems

### Observation

Civilization systems continue operating through interconnected economic, social, technological, institutional, environmental, and infrastructure systems that support organized human activity across different geographic scales.

Their functioning depends on relationships among populations, resources, knowledge, institutions, production systems, communication networks, infrastructure, governance, and the natural environment.

### DGCP™ Insight

Civilization systems continue emerging from the interaction of multiple interconnected systems that collectively support organized human activity and societal continuity.

---

## 122 — Global Interdependence

### Observation

Global interdependence continues connecting economies, societies, institutions, infrastructure, technologies, resources, and supply networks across national and regional boundaries.

Activities within one part of the global system can remain connected to capabilities, resources, information, infrastructure, and decisions located elsewhere through trade, finance, communication, transportation, energy, and institutional networks.

### DGCP™ Insight

Global interdependence continues reflecting structural relationships through which systems across different regions depend on shared networks, resources, capabilities, and information flows.

---

## 123 — Future Readiness

### Observation

Future readiness reflects the development and maintenance of capabilities that allow organizations and systems to operate under evolving conditions without requiring certainty about future outcomes.

These capabilities depend on reliable observation, knowledge, adaptable structures, institutional learning, operational capacity, coordination, resilience, and mechanisms for reviewing changing conditions.

### DGCP™ Insight

Future readiness continues emerging from capabilities that support observation, learning, adaptation, coordination, and resilient operation under changing conditions.
