# DGCP™ Daily Public Signal

## 118  Continuous Improvement

### Observation

Continuous improvement continues refining processes, workflows, controls, and operational practices through repeated observation, evaluation, learning, and documented adjustment.

Improvement processes depend on reliable information, feedback mechanisms, measurable objectives, defined responsibilities, organizational learning, and mechanisms for implementing and reviewing changes.

### DGCP™ Insight

Continuous improvement continues supporting system development by connecting observation, evaluation, learning, adjustment, and review within recurring operational cycles.

## 119  Operational Excellence

### Observation

Operational excellence reflects the structured coordination of people, processes, technology, information, resources, and governance in support of consistent and effective operations.

Its development depends on defined processes, reliable information, appropriate capabilities, operational discipline, measurement, accountability, learning, and continuing improvement.

### DGCP™ Insight

Operational excellence continues emerging from coordinated capabilities that support consistent, measurable, and continuously improving system operations.

---

## 120  Resilience

### Observation

Resilience reflects the capacity of systems and organizations to maintain essential functions, absorb disruption, adapt to changing conditions, and restore operations when affected.

Resilience depends on interconnected capabilities including redundancy, resources, information, coordination, continuity arrangements, adaptive capacity, institutional learning, and recovery mechanisms.

### DGCP™ Insight

Resilience continues reflecting the combined capacity of interconnected systems to maintain, adapt, recover, and continue essential functions under changing conditions.
