# DGCP™ Daily Public Signal

## 157: System Stability

### Observation

System stability continues reflecting the ability of systems to maintain recognizable functions, relationships, and operating conditions while responding to internal and external changes.

Stability can depend on system structure, feedback mechanisms, available capacity, resources, dependencies, coordination, operational controls, and the interactions among interconnected components.

### DGCP™ Insight

System stability continues reflecting the capacity of interconnected components and relationships to maintain system functions within changing operating conditions.

---

## 158: Operational Stability

### Observation

Operational stability continues describing the ability of organizations, infrastructure, processes, and services to maintain consistent operational functions under varying conditions.

Operational stability can depend on resource availability, workforce capability, reliable infrastructure, service continuity, information flows, coordination, maintenance, operating procedures, and mechanisms for responding to changing conditions.

### DGCP™ Insight

Operational stability continues reflecting the consistency of operational functions supported by coordinated resources, processes, infrastructure, information, and capabilities.

---

## 159: Structural Stability

### Observation

Structural stability continues reflecting the ability of system structures, relationships, interfaces, and supporting arrangements to retain their functional organization under changing conditions.

Structural stability can depend on the configuration of components, connections, dependencies, boundaries, network relationships, supporting infrastructure, and mechanisms that maintain interactions among system elements.

### DGCP™ Insight

Structural stability continues reflecting the persistence of functional relationships and system organization across changing conditions.
