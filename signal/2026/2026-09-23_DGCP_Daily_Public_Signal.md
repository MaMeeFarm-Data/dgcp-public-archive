# DGCP™ Daily Public Signal

## 190: Traceability

### Observation

Traceability continues providing structured relationships through which information, records, activities, components, decisions, and outputs can be connected to their documented origins, changes, and relevant processes.

Traceability can depend on identifiers, timestamps, records, references, version histories, documentation, linked evidence, and consistent methods for maintaining relationships across system activities.

### DGCP™ Insight

Traceability continues supporting structured system understanding by preserving connections among records, activities, changes, decisions, and their documented origins.

---

## 191: Provenance

### Observation

Provenance continues documenting the origin, history, custody, transformation, and contextual relationships associated with information, records, data, assets, and other system outputs.

Provenance can depend on source identification, timestamps, authorship or responsible entities, version records, transformation histories, documentation, and preserved relationships between originating material and subsequent outputs.

### DGCP™ Insight

Provenance continues supporting evidence integrity by preserving documented information about origin, history, transformation, and contextual relationships.

---

## 192: Chain of Evidence

### Observation

Chain of evidence continues connecting related records, observations, sources, transformations, verification activities, and resulting outputs through documented and traceable relationships.

A maintained chain can depend on identifiable records, timestamps, provenance information, documented transitions, reference links, custody information where relevant, and mechanisms for preserving relationships between evidence across different stages.

### DGCP™ Insight

Chain of evidence continues supporting evidence continuity by maintaining traceable relationships from originating records through subsequent processes and documented outputs.
