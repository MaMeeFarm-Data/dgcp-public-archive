- Employment conditions remained connected to broader economic activity and operating conditions.

### Energy Systems

- Energy systems continued supporting transportation, manufacturing, utilities, communication networks, and digital infrastructure.
- Oil, natural gas, electricity, and renewable energy systems remained active components of global economic activity.
- Energy supply and distribution continued supporting industrial and transportation activity.
- Electricity infrastructure remained connected to the continued operation and expansion of large-scale digital infrastructure.

- Artificial intelligence infrastructure activity remained observable across technology and infrastructure systems.

### Financial Systems

- Banking systems continued supporting payments, lending, settlement, and liquidity functions.
- Capital markets remained active across equity, bond, currency, and other financial segments.
- Financing activity continued supporting corporate, infrastructure, and public-sector operations.
