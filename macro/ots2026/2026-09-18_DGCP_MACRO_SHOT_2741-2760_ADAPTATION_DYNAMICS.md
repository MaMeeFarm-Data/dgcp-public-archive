### DGCP™ Macro Shot #2741: Adaptation Dynamics

Adaptation dynamics describe how systems modify operations, structures, relationships, or resource use in response to changing conditions.

Adaptation can occur through changes in behavior, processes, technologies, capacity, resources, pathways, timing, institutions, or network configuration.

Different system components can adapt at different rates and through different mechanisms.

### DGCP™ Macro Shot #2742: Operational Adaptation

Operational adaptation occurs when existing activities are modified to function under changing conditions.

Production schedules, transport operations, maintenance practices, processing sequences, service delivery, and resource allocation can be adjusted.

Operational changes can occur without altering the underlying purpose of the system.

Operational adaptation remains observable.

### DGCP™ Macro Shot #2743: Resource Adaptation

Resource adaptation occurs when systems change how resources are sourced, allocated, stored, processed, or used.

Energy, water, materials, labor, capital, land, information, and other inputs can be redistributed across activities.

Changing resource arrangements alter the relationship between available inputs and system functions.

Resource adaptation remains observable.

### DGCP™ Macro Shot #2744: Capacity Adaptation

Capacity adaptation occurs when systems modify available capability in response to changing activity or operating conditions.

Capacity can be expanded, contracted, redistributed, mobilized, reserved, or reassigned across components and locations.

Changes in capacity alter how much activity different parts of the system can accommodate.

### DGCP™ Macro Shot #2745: Infrastructure Adaptation

Infrastructure adaptation occurs when physical or digital infrastructure is modified, expanded, repurposed, connected, or operated differently.

Transport networks, energy systems, communications, storage, water infrastructure, computing systems, and logistics facilities can undergo operational or structural modification.

Infrastructure adaptation changes the physical or digital foundation supporting system activity.

### DGCP™ Macro Shot #2754: Incremental Adaptation

Incremental adaptation occurs through a sequence of limited modifications rather than a single large structural change.

### DGCP™ Macro Shot #2756: Distributed Adaptation

Distributed adaptation occurs when multiple components or participants adjust independently across a wider system.

### DGCP™ Macro Shot #2758: Cross-System Adaptation

### DGCP™ Macro Shot #2759: Adaptation Reconfiguration

Adaptation reconfiguration occurs when accumulated adjustments change the distribution of resources, capacity, pathways, dependencies, or functions across a system.

Repeated changes can shift activity toward different technologies, locations, networks, suppliers, processes, or operating structures.

Adaptation can therefore modify the architecture through which subsequent system activity occurs.

### DGCP™ Macro Shot #2760: Adaptation Dynamics Framework

Adaptation dynamics connect operations, resources, capacity, infrastructure, technology, processes, pathways, time, space, networks, behavior, and institutions.

Incremental and structural adaptation describe different scales of change while distributed and coordinated adaptation describe different organizational patterns.

Cross-system adaptation connects adjustments across dependencies while adaptation reconfiguration describes how accumulated changes can alter wider system architecture.

Together these relationships reveal how systems modify operations and structures as conditions change while redistributing activity across interconnected components and domains.
