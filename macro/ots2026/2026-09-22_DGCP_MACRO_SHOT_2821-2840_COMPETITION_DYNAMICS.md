### DGCP™ Macro Shot #2821: Competition Dynamics

Competition dynamics describe how multiple participants, systems, technologies, locations, or activities interact when seeking access to limited resources, capacity, demand, infrastructure, attention, or opportunities.

Competition can occur within markets, networks, institutions, supply systems, geographic regions, and technological environments.

Competitive relationships can change as availability, capacity, access, demand, technology, and system conditions change.

### DGCP™ Macro Shot #2840: Competition Dynamics Framework

Competition dynamics connect resources, capacity, infrastructure, inputs, suppliers, demand, markets, technologies, networks, routes, locations, time, access, and interfaces.
