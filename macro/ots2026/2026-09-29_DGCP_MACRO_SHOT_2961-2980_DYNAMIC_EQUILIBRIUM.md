### DGCP™ Macro Shot #2961: Dynamic Equilibrium

Dynamic equilibrium describes a system condition in which continuous changes occur while broader relationships remain within a recognizable operating range.

Inputs, outputs, resources, flows, capacity, behavior, and system interactions can change continuously without producing a fixed static state.

Equilibrium therefore can emerge through ongoing adjustment among changing system components.

Input output balance describes the relationship between what enters a system and what leaves it.
