Dependencies are relationships in which a system, process, component, or outcome relies on another condition, resource, component, or system.

A dependency may involve information, infrastructure, resources, services, people, processes, technology, timing, or other system conditions.

Identifying dependencies helps reveal what must remain available, functional, or coordinated for a system to operate as intended.

Observation identifies the relationship.

Evidence supports what can be verified.

Context helps explain how the dependency operates.

Dependencies connect system components and conditions.

A change in one dependency may affect connected parts of the system.

Feedback provides information about what followed.

Recording dependencies with time, context, and relevant evidence supports traceability and helps clarify relationships between system conditions and observed outcomes.

---

## Principle

Observe the relationship.

Identify the dependency.

Record what relies on what.

Preserve the context.

Maintain traceability.

Review dependencies when system conditions change.
