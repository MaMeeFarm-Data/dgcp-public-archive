Constraints are conditions or limits that restrict what a system can do, how it can operate, or which outcomes are possible.

A constraint may arise from available resources, capacity, time, rules, physical conditions, dependencies, technology, or other system conditions.

Identifying constraints helps distinguish between what is possible within the current system and what is limited by existing conditions.

Observation identifies the condition.

Evidence supports the observation.

Context helps define the constraint.

The constraint limits available actions or system behavior.

Responses may operate within the constraint, adapt to it, or change relevant system conditions.

Feedback provides information about what followed.

Recording constraints with time, context, and relevant evidence supports traceability and understanding of system behavior.

---

## Principle

Observe the condition.

Identify the constraint.

Record what limits the system.

Preserve the context.

Maintain traceability.

Learn from how the system responds.
