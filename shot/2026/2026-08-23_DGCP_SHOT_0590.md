Recovery paths describe the defined or available routes through which a component, process, function, or system may move from a disrupted or degraded state toward an operational state.

A recovery path may involve restoration, restart, replacement, failover, reconfiguration, resource substitution, manual intervention, data recovery, dependency restoration, or another response appropriate to the observed condition.

Identifying recovery paths helps clarify which responses are available, what conditions are required, which dependencies are involved, and what evidence is needed to determine whether recovery has occurred.

Observation identifies the current condition.

Evidence supports what can be verified.

Context helps explain the conditions surrounding the disruption and recovery.

System state provides information about conditions before, during, and after recovery actions.

Failure modes help identify the observed form of failure without by themselves establishing its underlying cause.

Dependencies help identify components, resources, services, or systems required for a particular recovery path.

Constraints may limit which recovery paths are available under current conditions.

A recovery action does not by itself establish successful recovery.

Verification is required to determine whether the relevant function, process, component, or system has returned to the defined operational condition.

Feedback provides information about what followed and may support further recovery actions or review.

Recording the disruption, available recovery paths, selected response, relevant dependencies and constraints, state changes, evidence, and verification results supports traceability and helps reconstruct how recovery proceeded.

---

## Principle

Observe the condition.

Identify the available recovery paths.

Record the relevant dependencies and constraints.

Select and record the response.

Observe the resulting state.

Verify the recovery.

Preserve the evidence and context.

Maintain traceability.

Review the recovery path when new evidence or system conditions emerge.
