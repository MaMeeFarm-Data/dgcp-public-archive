Triggers are conditions, signals, or events that initiate a defined response within a system.

A trigger may arise from an observed change, a threshold being reached, a scheduled condition, an external event, or another defined system condition.

Clear trigger definitions help distinguish when a response should begin and what condition initiated it.

Observation identifies the condition.

Evidence supports the observation.

Rules define when the condition becomes a trigger.

The trigger initiates a response.

The response may change the system state.

Feedback provides information about what followed.

Recording triggers with time, context, and relevant evidence supports traceability between observed conditions and subsequent system responses.

---

## Principle

Observe the condition.

Identify the trigger.

Record what initiated the response.

Preserve the context.

Maintain traceability.

Learn from what follows.
