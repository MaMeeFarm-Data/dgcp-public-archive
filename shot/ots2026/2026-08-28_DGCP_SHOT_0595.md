Adaptability describes the ability of a system, process, component, or function to adjust its behavior, configuration, structure, or operating method in response to changing conditions.

Adaptation may occur in response to changes in inputs, environment, resources, constraints, dependencies, demand, system state, or other relevant conditions.

Identifying adaptability helps clarify how a system can continue operating when existing conditions no longer support the same behavior or configuration.

Observation identifies the changes and responses that occur.

Evidence supports what can be verified.

Context helps explain the conditions surrounding the adjustment.

System state provides information about conditions before, during, and after adaptation.

Inputs, constraints, dependencies, interfaces, and available resources may influence which adjustments are possible.

Feedback may provide information that informs subsequent adjustments.

Adaptation may involve changing parameters, reallocating resources, modifying processes, switching dependencies, altering interfaces, or selecting different operating paths.

Adaptability does not mean that every change can be accommodated.

A system may adapt successfully within some conditions and reach limits under others.

Adaptation may also introduce new dependencies, constraints, trade-offs, or failure modes that require observation and verification.

Verification helps determine whether the adjusted system continues to perform its required function under the new conditions.

Recording the original condition, observed change, adaptation action, resulting state, relevant dependencies, constraints, evidence, and verification results supports traceability and helps distinguish observed adaptation from assumptions about future capability.

---

## Principle

Observe the change.

Identify what requires adjustment.

Record the available response.

Apply or observe the adaptation.

Record the resulting state.

Identify new dependencies or constraints.

Preserve the evidence and context.

Verify continued function.

Maintain traceability.

Review adaptability when system conditions change again.
