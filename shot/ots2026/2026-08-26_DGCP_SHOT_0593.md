Reliability describes the ability of a system, process, component, or service to perform its defined function under specified conditions for a defined period or sequence of operation.

Reliability may be observed through successful operation, consistent outputs, availability of required functions, failure frequency, recovery behavior, or other measurable characteristics relevant to the system being examined.

Identifying reliability helps distinguish repeated successful operation from isolated performance and helps clarify the conditions under which a function can be depended upon.

Observation identifies the behavior and conditions that occur.

Evidence supports what can be verified.

Context helps explain the operating conditions surrounding each observation.

System state provides information about conditions before, during, and after operation.

Failure modes help identify how reliability is lost when expected function is not maintained.

Dependencies and interfaces may influence whether required functions remain available.

Constraints may affect the conditions under which reliable operation is possible.

Recovery paths describe how operation may be restored after disruption or failure.

Reliability does not mean that failure is impossible.

Observed reliability under one set of conditions does not establish reliability under all conditions.

Verification helps determine whether the defined function was performed according to the criteria and conditions being examined.

Recording operating conditions, successful and failed operations, relevant dependencies, state changes, recovery actions, evidence, and verification results supports traceability and helps distinguish observed reliability from assumptions about future performance.

---

## Principle

Define the expected function.

Define the operating conditions.

Observe repeated operation.

Record successful and failed outcomes.

Identify relevant dependencies and constraints.

Preserve the evidence and context.

Verify against defined criteria.

Do not generalize beyond the observed conditions.

Maintain traceability.
