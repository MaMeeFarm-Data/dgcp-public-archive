Robustness describes the ability of a system, process, component, or function to continue operating within defined limits when conditions vary or disturbances occur.

Robustness may be observed when a system maintains acceptable function despite changes in inputs, load, environment, timing, dependencies, interfaces, or other operating conditions.

Identifying robustness helps clarify which variations a system can tolerate without losing required function or moving outside defined operating limits.

Observation identifies the behavior and conditions that occur.

Evidence supports what can be verified.

Context helps explain the variations or disturbances surrounding the observation.

System state provides information about conditions before, during, and after the disturbance.

Inputs, constraints, dependencies, and interfaces may influence how the system responds.

Control mechanisms, redundancy, buffering, alternative paths, or other structural features may contribute to continued operation under changing conditions.

Robustness does not mean that a system can tolerate every disturbance.

A system may remain robust within one range of conditions and fail outside that range.

Robustness also does not imply that performance remains unchanged. Degraded performance may still remain within defined acceptable limits.

Verification helps determine whether required function was maintained under the conditions being examined.

Recording disturbances, operating conditions, state changes, outputs, dependencies, responses, evidence, and verification results supports traceability and helps distinguish observed robustness from assumptions about untested conditions.

---

## Principle

Define the required function.

Define the acceptable operating limits.

Observe the disturbance or variation.

Record the system response.

Identify relevant dependencies and constraints.

Compare behavior with defined criteria.

Preserve the evidence and context.

Verify what remained functional.

Do not generalize beyond tested or observed conditions.

Maintain traceability.
