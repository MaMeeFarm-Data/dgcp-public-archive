### DGCP™ Country Scan #2761: Nepal

Nepal operates a landlocked economy whose international goods movement depends on transit through neighboring countries.

Road and rail connections through India provide major links between domestic markets and maritime gateways.

Northern routes through China provide additional external connections across Himalayan geography.

Border crossings, highways, rail connections, inland logistics facilities, and neighboring port systems support these flows.

Landlocked trade dependency remains observable.

External transit connections continue.
