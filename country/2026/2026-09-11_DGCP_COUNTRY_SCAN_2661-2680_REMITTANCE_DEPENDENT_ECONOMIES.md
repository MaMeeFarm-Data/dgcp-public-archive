### DGCP™ Country Scan #2661: Nepal

Nepal operates an economy with substantial structural connections to international labor migration and remittance flows.

Workers abroad connect household income, consumption, savings, finance, and domestic economic activity with employment systems outside the country.

Banks, money-transfer services, digital payment systems, migration networks, and international financial channels support these cross-border flows.

Remittances therefore connect external labor markets with multiple parts of the domestic economy.

Remittance dependency remains observable.

External income connections continue.
