### DGCP™ Country Scan #2501: Saudi Arabia

Saudi Arabia operates within an arid environment where water availability is structurally connected to economic and infrastructure systems.

Municipal supply, industry, agriculture, and urban development interact with desalination, groundwater, treated wastewater, storage, and distribution networks.

Energy infrastructure supports large-scale desalination and water movement across population and economic centers.

Water supply therefore connects natural resource conditions with engineered infrastructure and energy systems.

Water constraint remains observable.

Water-system dependencies continue.
