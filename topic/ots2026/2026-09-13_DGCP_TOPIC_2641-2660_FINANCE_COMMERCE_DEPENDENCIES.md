### DGCP™ Topic #2641: Finance–Commerce Dependencies

Finance–commerce dependencies connect the exchange of goods and services with payment, credit, banking, settlement, liquidity, insurance, and financial infrastructure.

Commercial transactions involve not only physical or digital exchange but also systems capable of transferring, recording, financing, and settling economic value.

Commerce therefore operates through relationships linking buyers, sellers, financial institutions, payment networks, markets, and supporting infrastructure.

Finance–commerce dependencies remain structurally observable.

I log finance–commerce dependencies.

### DGCP™ Topic #2642: Payment–Commerce Dependency

Payment–commerce dependency connects commercial transactions with systems used to transfer value between buyers and sellers.

Cash, bank transfers, cards, digital payments, and other payment arrangements operate through different combinations of institutions, networks, accounts, infrastructure, and settlement mechanisms.

Commercial exchange therefore depends on payment structures capable of completing the financial side of transactions.

Payment–commerce dependency remains structurally observable.

Businesses use banking infrastructure to receive revenue, pay suppliers and workers, manage funds, access financing, and conduct transactions.

Working capital commerce dependency connects daily business operations with financial resources used across inventory, receivables, payables, wages, procurement, and operating expenses.

Settlement–commerce dependency connects completed commercial transactions with systems that finalize financial obligations between participating parties.

Payments can pass through multiple institutions, accounts, networks, clearing processes, and settlement arrangements before financial obligations are completed.

Commerce therefore depends not only on transaction initiation but also on infrastructure that completes transfers of value.

### DGCP™ Topic #2653: Clearing–Commerce Dependency

Clearing–commerce dependency connects financial transactions with processes that transmit, reconcile, calculate, or organize obligations before settlement.

Banks, payment systems, financial institutions, processors, and clearing arrangements can form intermediate layers between payment initiation and final settlement.
