### DGCP™ Topic #2941: Recovery & Replacement Capacity

Recovery and replacement capacity connects existing system functions with the resources, equipment, workforce, infrastructure, and institutional capability available to restore or substitute physical and operational capacity.

Recovery can involve returning existing assets to operation, while replacement can involve introducing different assets capable of performing related functions.

Installed capacity therefore remains distinct from the wider system capacity available to restore, repair, substitute, or reproduce operating capability.

Recovery and replacement capacity remains structurally observable.

I log recovery and replacement capacity.
