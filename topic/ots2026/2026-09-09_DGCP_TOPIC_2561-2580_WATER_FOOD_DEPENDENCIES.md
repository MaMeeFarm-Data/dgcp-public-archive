### DGCP™ Topic #2561: Water–Food Dependencies

Water–food dependencies connect freshwater resources, water infrastructure, agricultural production, food processing, storage, distribution, and consumption systems.

Crop cultivation, livestock production, fisheries, food processing, cleaning, cooling, and other food-system activities interact with water at different stages.

Water availability, access, quality, timing, infrastructure, and management connect physical water systems with food production and distribution structures.

Water–food dependencies remain structurally observable.

### DGCP™ Topic #2562: Irrigation Water Dependency

Irrigation water dependency connects crop production with surface water, groundwater, reservoirs, canals, pumps, distribution systems, and field-level irrigation infrastructure.

Irrigated agriculture moves water from natural or engineered sources toward cultivated land through multiple storage and delivery structures.

The resulting relationship connects agricultural production with water availability, energy, infrastructure, and operating systems.

### DGCP™ Topic #2563: Rainfed Agriculture Water Dependency

Rainfed agriculture water dependency connects crop production with precipitation, soil moisture, seasonal water availability, and local hydrological conditions.

Unlike irrigated systems, water reaches cultivated land primarily through rainfall rather than engineered irrigation delivery.

Agricultural production therefore remains connected to the timing, distribution, and availability of water within local climatic and soil systems.

Rainfed agriculture water dependency remains structurally observable.
