### DGCP™ Topic #2301 — Circular Economy Systems

Circular economy systems connect resource extraction, production, distribution, use, maintenance, reuse, recovery, recycling, and secondary material flows.

Products and materials move through multiple operational stages before and after their primary use.

Circular structures create relationships between production systems, consumption systems, and material recovery networks.

### DGCP™ Topic #2302 — Circular Material Flows

Circular material flows describe the movement of materials through production, use, recovery, processing, and subsequent applications.

Metals, polymers, glass, paper, textiles, biological materials, and other resources follow different material pathways.

Recovered materials may reconnect downstream waste systems with upstream production processes.

Circular material flows remain structurally observable.

### DGCP™ Topic #2309 — Remanufacturing Systems

Remanufacturing systems process used products or components through structured industrial operations before returning them to functional use.

Remanufacturing connects recovered products with manufacturing and component supply systems.

### DGCP™ Topic #2310 — Product Disassembly Systems

Product disassembly systems separate products into components, assemblies, and materials after or during periods of use.

Manual processes, specialized tools, machinery, technical information, identification systems, and material handling support disassembly activities.

Separated components may enter reuse, refurbishment, remanufacturing, recycling, or disposal pathways.

Product disassembly systems remain structurally observable.

I log product disassembly systems.

Inspection, identification, removal, testing, grading, storage, documentation, and distribution form interconnected recovery activities.

Recovered components connect existing product stocks with repair, refurbishment, and manufacturing networks.
