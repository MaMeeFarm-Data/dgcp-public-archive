### DGCP™ Topic #2541: Energy–Industry Dependencies

Energy–industry dependencies connect electricity, fuels, heat, energy infrastructure, and industrial production systems.

Industrial activities require energy for machinery, processing, heating, cooling, pumping, compression, material handling, environmental control, and supporting operations.

Energy systems simultaneously interact with industrial producers that manufacture equipment, materials, components, and infrastructure used throughout energy networks.

Energy–industry dependencies remain structurally observable.
