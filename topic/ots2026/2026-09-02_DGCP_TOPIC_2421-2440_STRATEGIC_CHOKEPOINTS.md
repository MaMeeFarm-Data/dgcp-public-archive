### DGCP™ Topic #2421: Strategic Chokepoints

Strategic chokepoints are locations, nodes, routes, facilities, or system interfaces through which concentrated flows pass within wider networks.

Chokepoints can emerge where geography, infrastructure, technology, capacity, access, or network architecture limits the number of available pathways.

Material, energy, transport, information, commercial, and operational flows can intersect with chokepoint structures across multiple systems.
