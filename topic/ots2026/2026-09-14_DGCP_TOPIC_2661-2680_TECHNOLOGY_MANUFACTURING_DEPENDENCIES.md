### DGCP™ Topic #2661: Technology–Manufacturing Dependencies

Technology–manufacturing dependencies connect technological systems with the physical production of materials, components, equipment, devices, and industrial infrastructure.

Digital and advanced technologies operate through manufactured hardware produced across multiple industrial stages.

Technology systems therefore depend on production networks extending from raw materials and processing to components, assembly, testing, and final equipment.

Technology–manufacturing dependencies remain structurally observable.

Semiconductors form physical components within computers, communications equipment, vehicles, industrial machinery, consumer electronics, and infrastructure systems.
