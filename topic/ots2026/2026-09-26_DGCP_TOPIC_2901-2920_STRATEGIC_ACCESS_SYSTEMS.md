### DGCP™ Topic #2901: Strategic Access Systems

Strategic access systems connect resources, infrastructure, technology, capacity, and services with the mechanisms through which participants can physically or institutionally reach and use them.

Ownership, availability, and access represent distinct structural conditions within interconnected systems.

Usable capacity therefore depends not only on whether an asset or resource exists but also on whether pathways connecting users with that capacity are operationally available.

Strategic access systems remain structurally observable.

I log strategic access systems.
