### DGCP™ Topic #2601: Data–Connectivity Dependencies

Data–connectivity dependencies connect the creation, movement, exchange, storage, processing, and use of data with communications infrastructure.

Digital systems depend on physical and logical networks that connect users, devices, applications, computing facilities, platforms, and organizations across geographic locations.

Fiber, wireless networks, satellites, subsea cables, network equipment, exchange points, and data infrastructure form interconnected pathways through which data moves.

Data–connectivity dependencies remain structurally observable.
