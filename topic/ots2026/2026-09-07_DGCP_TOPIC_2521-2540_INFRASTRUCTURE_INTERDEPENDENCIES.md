### DGCP™ Topic #2521: Infrastructure Interdependencies

Infrastructure interdependencies occur when the operation of one infrastructure system depends on inputs, services, connections, or capabilities provided by another infrastructure system.

Electricity, water, transport, communications, digital infrastructure, energy supply, logistics, and other systems interact through shared physical and operational relationships.

These connections create infrastructure networks in which individual systems operate as parts of wider interconnected structures.

Infrastructure interdependencies remain structurally observable.

I log infrastructure interdependencies.
