### DGCP™ Topic #2501: Cross-Border Dependencies

Cross-border dependencies occur when resources, infrastructure, production, services, institutions, or operating systems in one jurisdiction depend on inputs, capabilities, connections, or functions located in another.

These relationships operate through trade, transportation, energy networks, digital connectivity, finance, technology, labor, and institutional arrangements.

National systems therefore interact with external structures through multiple physical, economic, technological, and institutional pathways.

Cross-border dependencies remain structurally observable.

I log cross-border dependencies.

### DGCP™ Topic #2502: Cross-Border Resource Dependencies

Cross-border resource dependencies connect resource-consuming systems with natural resources extracted or produced in other jurisdictions.

Minerals, metals, fuels, timber, agricultural commodities, and other primary materials move from resource locations through processing, transport, trade, and distribution networks.

The geography of resource availability can therefore connect domestic production systems with external extraction and supply structures.

Cross-border resource dependencies remain structurally observable.
