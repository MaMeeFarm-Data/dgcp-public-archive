### DGCP™ Topic #2461: Geographic Concentration

Geographic concentration occurs when resources, infrastructure, production, capacity, services, or specialized functions are clustered within limited geographic areas.

Concentration can emerge from resource locations, physical geography, infrastructure development, accumulated capabilities, market structures, or institutional environments.

Distributed systems can therefore depend on activities and capacities located within relatively concentrated regions.

Geographic concentration remains structurally observable.

Minerals, metals, energy resources, forests, agricultural land, freshwater, and other natural resources reflect underlying geological, climatic, and ecological conditions.
