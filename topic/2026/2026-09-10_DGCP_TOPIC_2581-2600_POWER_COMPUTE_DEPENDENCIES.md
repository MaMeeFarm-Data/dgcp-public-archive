### DGCP™ Topic #2581: Power–Compute Dependencies

Power–compute dependencies connect electricity systems with computing infrastructure, data centers, servers, processors, storage, networking, cooling, and digital operations.

Compute systems require electrical energy to operate hardware, facility infrastructure, thermal-management systems, communications equipment, and supporting services.

Electricity generation, transmission, distribution, and facility-level power systems therefore form physical infrastructure beneath digital computing capacity.

Power–compute dependencies remain structurally observable.
