### DGCP™ Topic #2921: Redundancy & Alternative Pathways

Redundancy and alternative pathways describe the presence of multiple assets, routes, suppliers, networks, facilities, or operating channels capable of supporting related system functions.

Alternative pathways can exist within physical infrastructure, production systems, logistics networks, digital systems, financial structures, and institutional arrangements.

System structure therefore includes not only primary operating pathways but also the distribution of function across parallel or alternative connections.

Redundancy and alternative pathways remain structurally observable.
