### DGCP™ Topic #2681: Resource–Production Dependencies

Resource–production dependencies connect natural resources and primary materials with the industrial systems that transform them into usable products.

Minerals, metals, energy resources, water, biomass, agricultural materials, and other physical inputs enter production through extraction, harvesting, processing, refining, transportation, and manufacturing stages.

Production systems therefore remain connected to upstream resource systems before materials reach factories and downstream markets.

Resource–production dependencies remain structurally observable.
