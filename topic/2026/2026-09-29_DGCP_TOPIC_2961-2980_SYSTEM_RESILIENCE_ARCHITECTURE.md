### DGCP™ Topic #2961: System Resilience Architecture

System resilience architecture describes the structural arrangement of capacity, access, redundancy, buffers, alternative pathways, recovery capability, and replacement capability within interconnected systems.

Resources, infrastructure, production, logistics, technology, finance, workforce, and institutions can each contain different structural layers supporting continued system function.

Resilience architecture therefore exists across relationships between system components rather than within a single asset or capacity source.
