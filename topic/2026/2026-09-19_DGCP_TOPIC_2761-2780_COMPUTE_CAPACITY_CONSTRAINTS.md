### DGCP™ Topic #2761: Compute Capacity Constraints

Compute capacity constraints occur where the physical and operational capacity of computing systems bounds the volume, scale, location, or type of workloads that can be executed.

Processors, accelerators, memory, storage, networking, electricity, cooling, facilities, and supporting infrastructure combine to form usable compute capacity.

Computational demand therefore remains distinct from the physical capacity available to execute digital workloads.

Compute capacity constraints remain structurally observable.

Processors operate within servers, clusters, data centers, edge systems, industrial equipment, and other computing environments.
