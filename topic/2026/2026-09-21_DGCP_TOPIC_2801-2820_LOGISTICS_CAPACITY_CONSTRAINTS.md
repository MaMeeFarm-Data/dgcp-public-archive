### DGCP™ Topic #2801: Logistics Capacity Constraints

Logistics capacity constraints occur where the physical and operational capacity of logistics systems bounds the movement, handling, storage, or distribution of goods.

Vehicles, vessels, terminals, warehouses, equipment, routes, labor, information systems, and supporting infrastructure combine to form usable logistics capacity.

The availability of goods and transport infrastructure therefore remains distinct from the capacity of logistics systems to move cargo through complete supply networks.

Logistics capacity constraints remain structurally observable.
