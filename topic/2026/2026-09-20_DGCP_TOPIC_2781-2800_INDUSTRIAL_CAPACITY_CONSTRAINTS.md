### DGCP™ Topic #2781: Industrial Capacity Constraints

Industrial capacity constraints occur where the physical and operational capacity of industrial systems bounds the volume, type, speed, or location of production.

Factories, machinery, utilities, materials, labor, transport, storage, technical systems, and supporting infrastructure combine to form usable industrial capacity.

Demand for industrial output therefore remains distinct from the productive capacity available to manufacture or process physical goods.

Industrial capacity constraints remain structurally observable.
