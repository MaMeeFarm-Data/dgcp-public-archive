### DGCP™ Topic #2841: Storage & Buffer Capacity

Storage and buffer capacity connects production, transport, processing, distribution, and consumption systems across differences in timing and flow.

Warehouses, tanks, silos, reservoirs, stockpiles, cold stores, terminals, batteries, and other storage structures hold materials or energy between system stages.

System throughput therefore depends not only on movement and production capacity but also on capacity to hold resources between connected activities.

Storage and buffer capacity remains structurally observable.
