### DGCP™ Topic #2361 — Demographic Systems

Demographic systems describe the structure, distribution, composition, and movement of human populations.

Population size, age, births, deaths, households, migration, geographic distribution, and other characteristics form interconnected demographic structures.

Demographic patterns connect population conditions with economic, social, infrastructural, and institutional systems.

Demographic systems remain structurally observable.

I log demographic systems.
