## Purpose

This public learning whiteboard explains the marketing cycle as a continuous process through which attention, experience, communication, and reputation can contribute to business value.

The objective is to help learners understand that marketing does not end when attention is created. Customer experience, opinion, sharing, trust, and long-term relationships continue to influence the cycle.

## Trigger

A product, message, or event enters public attention.

The trigger creates an opportunity for people to notice and engage with the subject.

---

## Attention

People notice the product, message, or event.

Curiosity and awareness may increase as information spreads.

---

## Discussion

People discuss, share, comment on, and react to what they observe.

These interactions can expand the conversation to additional audiences.

---

## Trial

Some people move from awareness to direct experience.

They try the product, service, or offering for themselves.

---

## Experience

Direct experience provides information that can shape individual opinions.

The resulting experience may be positive, negative, or mixed.

---

## Opinion & Sharing

People may communicate their experiences through reviews, conversations, photographs, videos, recommendations, or other forms of sharing.

Both positive and negative experiences can generate additional attention.

The difference lies in the resulting perception and outcome.

---

## Brand Awareness

Continued discussion and sharing can increase awareness of a brand, product, service, or topic.

Awareness expands the potential reach of the marketing cycle.

---

## Business Impact

Marketing activity may contribute to leads, sales, repeat customers, relationships, and long-term business value.

Business outcomes remain dependent on multiple factors beyond attention alone.

---

## Key Factors

Factors that can influence the marketing cycle include:

- Product Value
- Communication
- Emotion
- Platforms
- Trust & Credibility
- Timing

These factors affect how attention develops into experience, reputation, and potential value.

---

## What Sustains the Business?

Long-term business activity can be supported by:

- Quality
- Value
- Relationships
- Reputation
- Adaptation

Attention can be short-term.

Trust, reputation, and demonstrated value develop over time.

---

## Key Message

Marketing is not only the creation of attention.

Attention can lead to experience.

Experience can lead to opinion.

Opinion can lead to sharing.

Sharing can create additional attention.

Long-term value depends on what the organization consistently delivers throughout the cycle.
