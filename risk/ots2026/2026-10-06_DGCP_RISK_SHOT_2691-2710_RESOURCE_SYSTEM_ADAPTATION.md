### DGCP™ Risk Shot #2691: Resource System Adaptation

Resource systems adapt when changes in availability, access, demand, infrastructure, cost structures, or operating conditions alter how resources are sourced, processed, distributed, stored, and used.

Adaptation can occur through substitution, diversification, efficiency changes, capacity adjustment, inventory restructuring, infrastructure modification, and altered patterns of consumption.
