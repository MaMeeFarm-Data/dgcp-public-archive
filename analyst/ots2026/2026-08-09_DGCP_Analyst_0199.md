### System Context

Alerting remains one of the most important yet least visible capabilities supporting modern civilization.

Infrastructure depends on alerting.

Organizations depend on alerting.

Institutions depend on alerting.

Civilization depends on systems capable of communicating significant conditions so that attention can be directed toward situations requiring awareness and response.

Many observers focus on notifications, alarms, warnings, messages, or status changes.

The discussion frequently centers on the notification itself.

The structural capability allowing significant conditions to be distinguished from continuous streams of observation and brought to focused attention receives less attention.

While reviewing developments across infrastructure, governance, healthcare, finance, logistics, manufacturing, scientific research, public administration, engineering, environmental observation, communication networks, and digital systems, I found myself paying less attention to individual notifications and more attention to the alerting systems enabling significant conditions to become actionable awareness.

Notifications remained visible.

Actionable awareness became visible.

The observation was not about notifications.

The observation was about alerting.
