### System Context

Norms remain one of the most important yet least visible structures supporting modern civilization.

Infrastructure operates within norms.

Organizations operate within norms.

Institutions operate within norms.

Civilization depends on systems capable of establishing shared expectations for conduct so that behavior can be coordinated even when every expectation is not expressed through formal rules.

Many observers focus on expectations, customs, conventions, accepted practices, appropriate behavior, social responses, or visible patterns of conduct.

The discussion frequently centers on what people are expected to do.

The structural capability through which shared expectations guide behavior across repeated social and institutional interactions receives less attention.

While reviewing developments across governance, education, healthcare, science, technology, economic systems, public administration, organizational systems, community structures, professional relationships, communication networks, and institutional environments, I found myself paying less attention to individual expectations and more attention to the normative structures guiding conduct across society.

Expectations remained visible.

Shared standards of conduct became visible.

The observation was not about expectations.

The observation was about norms.
