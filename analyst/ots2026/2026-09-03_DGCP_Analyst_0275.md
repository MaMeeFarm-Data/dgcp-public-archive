### System Context

Expertise remains one of the most important yet least visible capabilities supporting modern civilization.

Professional systems depend on expertise.

Organizations depend on expertise.

Institutions depend on expertise.

Civilization depends on systems capable of developing accumulated mastery through knowledge, experience, and practice so that specialized capability can support complex system functions.

Many observers focus on specialists, experts, professions, qualifications, credentials, technical fields, experience, or other visible indicators associated with specialized knowledge and capability.

The discussion frequently centers on who the experts are.

The structural capability through which accumulated knowledge, experience, and practice develop into mastery receives less attention.

While reviewing developments across science, healthcare, engineering, technology, agriculture, manufacturing, transportation, infrastructure, energy, education, public administration, professional systems, organizational environments, and institutional systems, I found myself paying less attention to individual specialists and more attention to the accumulated mastery supporting complex system functions.

Specialists remained visible.

Accumulated mastery became visible.

The observation was not about specialists.

The observation was about expertise.
