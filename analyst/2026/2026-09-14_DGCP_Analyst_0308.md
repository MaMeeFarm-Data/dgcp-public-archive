### System Context

Supplier governance remains one of the most important yet least visible structures supporting modern civilization.

Organizations depend on supplier governance.

Institutions depend on supplier governance.

Supply systems depend on supplier governance.

Civilization depends on systems capable of establishing requirements and oversight for supply relationships so that expected supplier performance can remain connected with reliable supply.

Many observers focus on vendors, suppliers, contracts, purchasing arrangements, delivery schedules, performance reports, evaluations, or other visible elements associated with supplier relationships.

The discussion frequently centers on individual vendors and their performance.

The structural capability through which requirements and oversight are established across supply relationships receives less attention.

While reviewing developments across governance, healthcare, education, infrastructure, manufacturing, logistics, technology, public administration, public procurement, organizational environments, and institutional systems, I found myself paying less attention to individual vendors and more attention to the governance structures organizing requirements and oversight across supply relationships.

Vendors remained visible.

Governed supply relationships became visible.

The observation was not about vendors.

The observation was about supplier governance.

Behind oversight of supply relationships exists supplier governance.

Behind structured supplier relationships exists supplier governance.

Behind recurring observation of supplier performance exists supplier governance.
