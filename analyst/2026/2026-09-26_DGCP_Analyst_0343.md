### System Context

Local capacity remains one of the most important yet least visible capabilities supporting modern civilization.

Communities depend on local capacity.

Local systems depend on local capacity.

Continuity under changing conditions can depend on local capacity.

Civilization depends on systems through which resources, knowledge, and capabilities can become available within or near communities so that needs can potentially be addressed close to where they emerge.

Many observers focus on local resources, facilities, organizations, people, equipment, knowledge, or other visible elements available within communities.

The discussion frequently centers on what resources exist locally.

The structural capability that can emerge when resources, knowledge, and capabilities become locally available and usable receives less attention.

While reviewing developments across communities, local organizations, services, production systems, institutions, households, and other areas where needs emerge close to everyday activity, I found myself paying less attention to individual local resources and more attention to the capability that can exist when resources, knowledge, and skills are available within or near local systems.

Local resources remained visible.

Capability close to where needs emerge became visible.

The observation was not about local resources.

The observation was about local capacity.
