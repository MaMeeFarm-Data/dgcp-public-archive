## Overview

Nursing is more than clinical care.

It is the system of caring for people across their health journey—promoting wellness, preventing illness, restoring health, and supporting dignity.

This public learning board introduces Nursing from a systems thinking perspective, emphasizing patients, families, healthcare teams, evidence and data, resources, policies, safety, ethics, health outcomes, and continuous learning.

---

## Learning Topics

1. What is Nursing?
2. Why Nursing?
3. Systems Thinking for Nursing
4. Nursing Care Process
5. High-Level Nursing System Architecture
6. Common Trade-offs
7. Nursing Risk Categories
8. Great Nurses
9. Nursing Values and Key Principles
