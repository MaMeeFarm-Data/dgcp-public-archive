## Overview

Project Management is more than planning schedules.

It is the discipline of leading people, managing resources, coordinating work, and balancing scope, time, cost, quality, and risk to deliver the right outcomes.

This public learning board introduces Project Management from a systems thinking perspective, emphasizing stakeholders, dependencies, constraints, communication, coordination, trade-offs, value delivery, and continuous improvement.

---

## Learning Topics

1. What is Project Management?
2. Why Project Management?
3. Systems Thinking for Project Management
4. Project Management Life Cycle
5. High-Level Project System Architecture
6. Good Project Managers
7. Common Trade-offs
8. Key Principles
9. Project Manager Mindset
