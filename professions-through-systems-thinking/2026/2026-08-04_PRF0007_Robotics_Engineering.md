## Overview

Robotics Engineering is more than building robots.

It is the discipline of designing intelligent physical systems that sense, think, act, and interact with the real world safely and effectively.

This public learning board introduces Robotics Engineering from a systems thinking perspective, emphasizing relationships between sensors, perception, planning, control, actuation, software, hardware, power systems, environment, safety, and continuous improvement.

## Learning Topics

1. What is Robotics Engineering?
2. Robotics vs Traditional Engineering
3. Systems Thinking for Robotics
4. Robotics Engineering Life Cycle
5. High-Level Robotic System Architecture
6. Good Robotic Systems
7. Common Trade-offs
8. Key Principles
9. Robotics Engineer Mindset
