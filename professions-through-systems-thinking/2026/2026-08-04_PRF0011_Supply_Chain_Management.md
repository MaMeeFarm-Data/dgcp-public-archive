## Overview

Supply Chain Management is more than moving goods.

It is the discipline of managing flows of products, information, and money across connected organizations from suppliers to customers to deliver value efficiently and sustainably.

This public learning board introduces Supply Chain Management from a systems thinking perspective, emphasizing end-to-end flows, dependencies, visibility, collaboration, resilience, sustainability, trade-offs, and continuous improvement.

## Learning Topics

2. Why Supply Chain Management?
3. Systems Thinking for Supply Chain
4. Supply Chain Management Life Cycle
5. High-Level Supply Chain System Architecture
6. Great Supply Chain Managers
7. Common Trade-offs
8. Key Principles
9. Supply Chain Professional Mindset
