## Overview

Investment Analysis is more than picking assets.

It is the discipline of analyzing opportunities, risks, and value to support informed capital allocation and long-term decision-making.

This public learning board introduces Investment Analysis from a systems thinking perspective, emphasizing economic and industry context, business models, financial analysis, risk, uncertainty, portfolio relationships, feedback, and continuous learning.

---

## Learning Topics

1. What is Investment Analysis?
2. Why Investment Analysis?
3. Systems Thinking for Investment Analysis
4. Investment Analysis Process
5. High-Level Investment System Architecture
6. Common Trade-offs
7. Key Principles
8. Investor Mindset
