## Overview

Civil engineering is more than building structures.

It is a system of planning, designing, constructing, operating, and maintaining infrastructure that supports communities, connects people, and enables economic and social development.

This public learning board introduces Civil Engineering from a systems thinking perspective, connecting people, infrastructure, design, construction, materials, safety, resilience, environment, public policy, operations, and long-term system performance.

---

## Learning Topics

1. What is Civil Engineering?
2. Why Civil Engineering?
3. Systems Thinking for Civil Engineering
4. Civil Engineering Process — Project Life Cycle
5. High-Level Civil Engineering System Architecture
6. Great Civil Engineers
7. Common Trade-offs
8. Key Principles
9. Civil Engineer Mindset
