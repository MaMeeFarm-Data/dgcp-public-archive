## Overview

Accounting is more than numbers.

It is the system of recording, classifying, summarizing, and reporting financial information to support decisions, accountability, and transparency.

This public learning board introduces Accounting from a systems thinking perspective, emphasizing information flow, financial reporting, management analysis, governance, compliance, organizational relationships, trust, and continuous improvement.

---

## Learning Topics

1. What is Accounting?
2. Why Accounting?
3. Systems Thinking for Accounting
4. Accounting Information Flow
5. High-Level Accounting System Architecture
6. Great Accountants
7. Common Trade-offs
8. Key Principles
9. Accountant Mindset
