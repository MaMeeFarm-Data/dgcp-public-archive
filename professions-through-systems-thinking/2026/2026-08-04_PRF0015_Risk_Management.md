## Overview

Risk Management is more than reacting to problems.

It is the discipline of understanding uncertainty, assessing potential impacts, and preparing systems to adapt, remain resilient, and continue delivering value.

This public learning board introduces Risk Management from a systems thinking perspective, emphasizing interdependencies, risk identification, assessment, response, monitoring, communication, resilience, and continuous improvement.
