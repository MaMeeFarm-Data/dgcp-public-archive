## Overview

DevOps Engineering is more than tools and automation.

It brings people, processes, and technology together to deliver value continuously, reliably, and safely.

This public learning board introduces DevOps Engineering from a systems thinking perspective, emphasizing delivery flow, collaboration, automation, CI/CD, infrastructure, observability, feedback, reliability, and continuous improvement.

---

## Learning Topics

1. What is DevOps Engineering?
2. Why DevOps?
3. Systems Thinking for DevOps
4. DevOps Engineering Life Cycle
5. DevOps System Architecture
6. Good DevOps Systems
7. Common Trade-offs
8. Key Principles
9. DevOps Engineer Mindset
