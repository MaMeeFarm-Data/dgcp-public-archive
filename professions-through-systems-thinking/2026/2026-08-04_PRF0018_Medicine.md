## Overview

Medicine is more than treating illness.

It is a system of understanding, preventing, diagnosing, treating, and caring for people to improve health and quality of life.

This public learning board introduces Medicine from a systems thinking perspective, connecting patients, medical professionals, hospitals, public health, laboratories, research, pharmacy, system resources, evidence, and continuous care.

---

## Learning Topics

1. What is Medicine?
2. Why Medicine?
3. Systems Thinking for Medicine
4. Medicine Process — Patient Journey
5. High-Level Medicine System Architecture
6. Great Medical Professionals
7. Common Trade-offs
8. Key Principles
9. Medicine Professional Mindset
