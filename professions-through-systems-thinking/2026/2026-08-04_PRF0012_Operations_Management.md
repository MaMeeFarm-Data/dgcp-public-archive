## Overview

Operations Management is more than running operations.

It is the discipline of designing, managing, and improving systems that transform inputs into valuable outputs efficiently, reliably, and sustainably.

This public learning board introduces Operations Management from a systems thinking perspective, emphasizing processes, people, technology, resources, capacity, customer value, performance measurement, trade-offs, and continuous improvement.

2. Why Operations Management?

8. Key Principles
9. Operations Manager Mindset
