## Overview

Public health is more than healthcare.

It is a system of protecting and improving the health of populations through prevention, promotion, policy, and collective action.

This public learning board introduces Public Health from a systems thinking perspective, connecting communities, healthcare systems, government and policy, environment and infrastructure, education and research, data and information, equity, and long-term population outcomes.

---

## Learning Topics

1. What is Public Health?
2. Why Public Health?
3. Systems Thinking for Public Health
4. Public Health Process
5. High-Level Public Health System Architecture
6. Great Public Health Professionals
7. Common Trade-offs
8. Key Principles
9. Public Health Mindset
