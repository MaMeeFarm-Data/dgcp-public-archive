## Overview

Business Analysis is more than documents or requirements.

It is the discipline of understanding problems, people, processes, information, systems, and goals to connect the pieces and create solutions that deliver value.

This public learning board introduces Business Analysis from a systems thinking perspective, emphasizing stakeholder relationships, dependencies, root causes, solution design, value delivery, outcomes, and continuous learning.

2. Why Business Analysis?
3. Systems Thinking for Business Analysis
4. Business Analysis Life Cycle
5. High-Level Business Analysis Architecture
6. Good Business Analysts
7. Common Trade-offs
8. Key Principles
9. Business Analyst Mindset
