## Overview

Product Management is more than building products or managing features.

It is the discipline of understanding users, opportunities, business goals, technology, data, and constraints to connect people and resources around the right outcomes.

This public learning board introduces Product Management from a systems thinking perspective, emphasizing the product ecosystem, user needs, stakeholder relationships, prioritization, value delivery, feedback loops, trade-offs, and continuous improvement.

8. Key Principles
9. Product Manager Mindset
