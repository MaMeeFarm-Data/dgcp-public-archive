## Overview

Banking is more than money.

It is a system that connects savers, borrowers, businesses, and institutions, manages risk, and supports economic activity through trust, capital, and liquidity.

This public learning board introduces Banking from a systems thinking perspective, emphasizing relationships across customers, regulators, financial markets, technology, infrastructure, risk management, operations, and the broader financial system.

---

## Learning Topics

1. What is Banking?
2. Why Banking?
3. Systems Thinking for Banking
4. Banking Value Chain
5. High-Level Banking System Architecture
6. Banking Risk Categories
7. Key Roles of Banks
8. Banking System Feedback Loops
9. Common Trade-offs
10. Core Principles
11. Trust and System Resilience
