## Issue

The external OpenTimestamps (OTS) web service was temporarily unavailable during scheduled timestamp operations for the DGCP Public Archive.

The service returned a 504 Gateway Timeout error, preventing the normal OTS workflow from being used at the time of observation.

---

## Operational Impact

Public archive operations continued normally.

The temporary interruption affected the normal external OTS workflow only.

No published document, archive record, or repository content was modified as a result of the interruption.

No internal DGCP repository failure was observed.

---

## Operational Response

DGCP™ continued scheduled archive operations without waiting for the OTS web service to become available.

DGI OTS Verification was used as a temporary operational substitute for applicable timestamp and verification operations during the observed OTS interruption.

Applicable records processed through DGI were preserved according to the normal DGCP™ archive procedure.

No duplicate OTS processing is required for records completed through the temporary DGI procedure solely because the OTS service later becomes available.

When normal OTS operation becomes available again, subsequent applicable timestamp operations may return to the established OTS workflow.

---

## Temporary Verification Procedure

During temporary OTS unavailability, the operational workflow is:

```text
Applicable Record
→ DGI OTS Verification
→ Hash / Verification Record
→ Archive Preservation
````

Under normal OTS availability, the established workflow remains:

```text
Applicable Record
→ OpenTimestamps
→ OTS Record
→ Archive Preservation
```

The temporary use of DGI provides operational continuity during OTS service interruption.

It does not require the same record to be processed through both services.

## Service Continuity

OpenTimestamps remains part of the normal DGCP™ timestamp workflow.

The temporary use of DGI does not discontinue or replace OTS as the established normal workflow.

DGI functions as an operational substitute when OTS is temporarily unavailable.

Once normal OTS operation becomes available, subsequent applicable records may continue through the established OTS process.

Records already completed through DGI during the interruption are not required to undergo duplicate OTS processing.
