### DGCP™ Corporate Scan #3041: BHP

BHP operates across major mineral resource systems including iron ore, copper, metallurgical coal, and potash development.

Its portfolio connects resource deposits with extraction capacity, processing systems, transportation infrastructure, customers, and capital allocation.

Resource system adaptation remains connected with commodity mix, asset configuration, mine development, operational productivity, and access to long-duration mineral resources.

The organization provides an observable example of how a diversified resource producer adjusts its operating structure around changing material requirements and portfolio conditions.

Conclusion: BHP represents resource system adaptation through portfolio configuration, production capacity, and long-duration mineral access.
