## Purpose

This educational journey explores wine as a global system connecting geography, agriculture, culture, production, logistics, trade, and time.

The objective is to observe how different landscapes, climates, grape-growing regions, winemaking traditions, infrastructure, and markets connect vineyards with consumers around the world.

---

## Journey Overview

**Global Route**

France → Italy → Spain → Georgia → South Africa → United States → Argentina → Australia

**Major Wine Regions**

- Bordeaux, France
- Tuscany, Italy
- Rioja, Spain
- Kakheti, Georgia
- Stellenbosch, South Africa
- Napa Valley, United States
- Mendoza, Argentina
- Barossa Valley, Australia

**System Journey**

Vineyard → Harvest → Winemaking → Aging → Bottling → Distribution → Market

## System Observation

Wine connects landscape, climate, agriculture, production systems, culture, logistics, and global markets.

Different regions combine environmental conditions with local knowledge, infrastructure, and established production practices to create distinct wine systems.

Following the route from vineyard to market provides a way to observe how agriculture, processing, transportation, heritage, and international trade interact across countries and generations.
