## Purpose

This educational journey explores Brazil through its large-scale coffee system.

The objective is to understand how geography, agriculture, production scale, infrastructure, processing, logistics, rural economies, and international trade connect Brazil's coffee-growing regions with global markets.

## System Observation

Brazil's coffee system demonstrates how production scale, geography, infrastructure, technology, logistics, and agricultural communities interact within a global commodity system.

Coffee-growing regions connect farms and processing facilities with road and rail networks, storage systems, ports, international markets, and consumers.

The system provides a useful perspective for observing how large-scale agricultural production becomes part of global supply and trade networks.
