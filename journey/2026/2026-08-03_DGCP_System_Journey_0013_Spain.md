## Purpose

This educational journey explores Spain through its wine system.

The objective is to understand how geography, terroir, indigenous grape varieties, viticulture, production traditions, infrastructure, logistics, tourism, culture, and global markets connect Spain's diverse wine regions.

## System Observation

Spain's wine system demonstrates how geography, climate, soil, indigenous grape varieties, agricultural knowledge, production traditions, infrastructure, and culture interact across distinct regions.

Regional wine systems connect vineyards and wineries with aging, quality control, transportation, tourism, hospitality, and international markets.

The diversity of Spanish wine provides a useful perspective for observing how regional identity, established traditions, and modern production systems remain connected within a global wine economy.
