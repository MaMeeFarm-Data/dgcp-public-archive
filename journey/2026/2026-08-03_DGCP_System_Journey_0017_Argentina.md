## Purpose

This educational journey explores Argentina through its wine system.

The objective is to understand how high-altitude geography, climate, water resources, terroir, viticulture, production infrastructure, logistics, tourism, innovation, and international markets connect Mendoza's wine regions with the wider global wine system.

## System Observation

Argentina's wine system demonstrates how high-altitude geography, desert climate, Andean water resources, agricultural knowledge, production infrastructure, and technology interact within a major wine-producing region.

Mendoza connects vineyards and wineries with irrigation systems, quality control, tourism, transportation, export networks, and international markets.

The system provides a useful perspective for observing how geography, water management, regional identity, production knowledge, and global trade remain connected within a modern wine economy.
