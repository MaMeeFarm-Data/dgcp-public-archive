## Purpose

This educational journey explores Italy through its wine system.

The objective is to understand how geography, terroir, viticulture, regional traditions, production infrastructure, logistics, tourism, culture, and global markets connect Italy's diverse wine regions.

## System Observation

Italy's wine system demonstrates how geography, climate, soil, grape varieties, agricultural knowledge, production traditions, infrastructure, and culture interact across highly diverse regions.

Regional wine systems connect vineyards and wineries with processing, aging, quality control, transportation, tourism, hospitality, and international markets.

The diversity of Italian wine provides a useful perspective for observing how local identity and long-established practices can remain connected to modern production and global trade systems.
