## Purpose

This educational journey explores Georgia through its ancient wine system.

The objective is to understand how geography, terroir, indigenous grape varieties, traditional qvevri winemaking, cultural heritage, infrastructure, tourism, and trade connect one of the world's oldest documented wine traditions with the modern wine system.

## System Observation

Georgia's wine system demonstrates how geography, agriculture, indigenous grape varieties, traditional knowledge, production methods, culture, and heritage can remain connected across thousands of years.

The qvevri tradition links vineyards and local winemaking practices with family cellars, cultural institutions, tourism, education, and modern export markets.

Georgia provides a useful perspective for observing how an ancient production system can remain active while interacting with contemporary infrastructure and global trade.
