## Purpose

This educational journey explores Vietnam through its coffee system.

The objective is to understand how geography, agriculture, Robusta production, processing infrastructure, rural communities, logistics, culture, and international trade connect Vietnam's coffee-growing regions with global markets.

## System Observation

Vietnam's coffee system demonstrates how agriculture, geography, processing capacity, infrastructure, logistics, rural communities, and consumption culture interact within a global commodity network.

Highland production regions connect farms and processing facilities with storage systems, transportation networks, ports, international markets, and the wider food and beverage industry.

Coffee provides a useful lens for observing how local agricultural systems can become deeply connected to global supply chains and everyday consumption.
