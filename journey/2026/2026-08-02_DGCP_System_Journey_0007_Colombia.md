## Purpose

This educational journey explores Colombia through its coffee system.

The objective is to understand how mountain geography, climate, agriculture, farming communities, processing infrastructure, logistics, culture, and international trade connect Colombian coffee-growing regions with global markets.

## System Observation

Colombia's coffee system demonstrates how geography, elevation, climate, agriculture, infrastructure, logistics, and human knowledge interact across a global value chain.

Mountain landscapes and diverse microclimates support different growing conditions, while farms, cooperatives, processing systems, transportation networks, ports, and international markets connect local production with consumers around the world.

Coffee therefore provides a useful lens for observing the relationship between natural systems, local communities, and global trade.
