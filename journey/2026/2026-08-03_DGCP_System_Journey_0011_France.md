## Purpose

This educational journey explores France through its wine system.

The objective is to understand how terroir, geography, climate, viticulture, winemaking traditions, infrastructure, regulation, logistics, culture, and global markets connect major French wine regions within a broader system.

## System Observation

France's wine system demonstrates how geography, climate, soil, agricultural knowledge, production practices, regulation, infrastructure, culture, and markets interact over time.

Distinct wine regions combine local environmental conditions with established knowledge and production systems, while appellation structures connect geographic origin with product identity.

Following these regional systems provides a way to observe how agriculture, heritage, regulation, logistics, tourism, and international markets remain connected within a long-established wine economy.
