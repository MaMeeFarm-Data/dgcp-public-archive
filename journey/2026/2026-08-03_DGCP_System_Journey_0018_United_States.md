## Purpose

This educational journey explores the United States through the Napa Valley wine system in California.

The objective is to understand how geography, terroir, viticulture, water management, production infrastructure, innovation, tourism, logistics, and international markets connect within one of the world's recognized wine regions.

## System Observation

Napa Valley demonstrates how geography, climate, soils, agricultural knowledge, production infrastructure, and regional specialization interact within a premium wine system.

Vineyards and wineries connect with water management, quality control, hospitality, tourism, transportation, distribution, technology, and international markets.

The system provides a useful perspective for observing how terroir, tradition, innovation, production, and market connectivity operate together within a globally recognized wine region.
