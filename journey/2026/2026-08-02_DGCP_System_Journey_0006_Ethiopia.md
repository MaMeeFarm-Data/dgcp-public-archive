## Purpose

This educational journey explores Ethiopia through the global system of coffee.

The objective is to understand how geography, biodiversity, agriculture, culture, infrastructure, processing, logistics, and international trade connect coffee-growing regions with consumers around the world.

## System Observation

Coffee begins long before it reaches a cup.

Ethiopia demonstrates how geography, biodiversity, agricultural production, processing systems, rural infrastructure, logistics, culture, and global trade can connect local production landscapes with international markets.

Following the coffee journey reveals the wider system behind one of the world's most recognized agricultural products.
