## Purpose

This educational journey explores South Africa through its wine system.

The objective is to understand how geography, terroir, viticulture, winemaking heritage, infrastructure, water management, logistics, tourism, innovation, and international markets connect South Africa's major wine regions.

## System Observation

South Africa's wine system demonstrates how diverse landscapes, coastal influences, soil conditions, agricultural knowledge, production infrastructure, and established winemaking traditions interact across distinct regions.

Regional wine systems connect vineyards and cellars with water management, quality control, transportation, tourism, research, sustainability practices, and international markets.

The system provides a useful perspective for observing how heritage, regional diversity, modern production, and global trade remain connected within an evolving wine economy.
