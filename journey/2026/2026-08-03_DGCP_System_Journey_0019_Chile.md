## Purpose

This educational journey explores Chile through its wine system.

The objective is to understand how geography, Pacific influence, the Andes Mountains, climate, soils, water resources, viticulture, production infrastructure, logistics, innovation, and international markets connect across Chile's major wine regions.

## System Observation

Chile's wine system demonstrates how the Andes Mountains, Pacific Ocean, valleys, soils, climate, water resources, and agricultural knowledge interact across geographically diverse production regions.

Vineyards and wineries connect with irrigation systems, quality control, tourism, hospitality, transportation, export networks, innovation, and international markets.

The system provides a useful perspective for observing how geography, terroir, production knowledge, infrastructure, sustainability, and global trade remain connected within a major New World wine economy.
