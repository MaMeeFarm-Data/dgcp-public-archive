### DGCP™ Politics Shot #2251: AI Compute Dependency

AI compute dependency exists when artificial intelligence development, training, inference, deployment, or operation relies on access to specialized computing capacity and the systems supporting it.

AI compute depends on processors, accelerators, memory, servers, data centers, electricity, cooling, networks, software, and technical expertise.

These capabilities can be distributed across multiple firms and jurisdictions.

AI compute dependency therefore extends beyond semiconductor availability alone.

AI compute dependency remains observable.

I log AI compute dependency.
