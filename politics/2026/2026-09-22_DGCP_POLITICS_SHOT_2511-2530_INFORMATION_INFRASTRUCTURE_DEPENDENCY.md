### DGCP™ Politics Shot #2511: Information Infrastructure Dependency

Information infrastructure dependency exists when political, economic, institutional, or social systems rely on interconnected infrastructure for creating, transmitting, storing, processing, or accessing information.

Information systems can depend on telecommunications networks, internet infrastructure, data centers, cloud platforms, satellites, software, electricity, and technical personnel.

Visible information services therefore operate above multiple physical and digital infrastructure layers.

Information infrastructure dependency requires observation of the systems beneath information access and movement.

Information infrastructure dependency remains observable.

I log information infrastructure dependency.
