### DGCP™ Politics Shot #2111: Political Control Points

Political control points are locations, institutions, infrastructures, rules, capabilities, or access mechanisms through which political authority can influence participation within a system.

Control can operate through ownership, jurisdiction, regulation, technical capability, institutional authority, or access conditions.

Different systems contain different forms of control and may involve several actors simultaneously.

Political control points therefore require structural mapping rather than assumptions about intent or use.

Political control points remain observable.

### DGCP™ Politics Shot #2112: Jurisdictional Control

Jurisdictional control exists where states or other legally recognized authorities possess formal authority over territory, institutions, activities, infrastructure, or participants.

Legal jurisdiction can determine which rules apply to organizations, transactions, technologies, resources, and infrastructure.

Cross-border systems can involve several jurisdictions at different operational layers.

Jurisdictional control therefore forms a foundational layer of political authority within interconnected systems.

### DGCP™ Politics Shot #2130: Political Control Point Architecture

Political control point architecture describes the interconnected locations, institutions, infrastructures, technologies, rules, resources, and access mechanisms through which authority and system participation are structured.

Control can arise from jurisdiction, territory, infrastructure, resources, technology, data, networks, logistics, finance, standards, or security systems.

Multiple actors may control different layers of the same dependency while also remaining dependent on other parts of the wider system.

The existence of a control point does not by itself establish coercion, political intent, effective leverage, or a specific political outcome.

Political control point architecture therefore requires separation of observable structural position from interpretation of how that position may be used.
