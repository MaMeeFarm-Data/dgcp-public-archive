### DGCP™ Politics Shot #2411: Defense Supply Dependency

Defense supply dependency exists when defense institutions and supporting industrial systems rely on external or concentrated sources of equipment, components, materials, technology, maintenance, or other inputs.

Defense supply systems connect industrial production with technology, minerals, energy, logistics, finance, workforce, and institutional procurement.

Possession of finished equipment does not necessarily establish independent capability across its supporting supply chain.

Defense supply dependency therefore requires observation across multiple industrial and operational layers.

Defense supply dependency remains observable.
