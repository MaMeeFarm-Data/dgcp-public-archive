### DGCP™ Politics Shot #2611: Strategic Geography Exposure

Strategic geography exposure exists when political, economic, infrastructure, or security systems depend on capabilities, resources, routes, or facilities located within particular geographic areas.

Geography can shape access, distance, connectivity, transport, jurisdiction, infrastructure placement, and resource distribution.

Systems that appear distributed organizationally can therefore remain connected through common geographic locations.

Strategic geography exposure requires observation of where critical system functions physically occur.

Strategic geography exposure remains observable.
