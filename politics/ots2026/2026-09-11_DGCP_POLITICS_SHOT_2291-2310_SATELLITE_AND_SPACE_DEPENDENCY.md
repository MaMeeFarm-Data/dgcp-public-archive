### DGCP™ Politics Shot #2291: Satellite & Space Dependency

Space-based capability therefore remains connected to extensive infrastructure on Earth.

### DGCP™ Politics Shot #2310: Satellite & Space Dependency Architecture

Satellite and space dependency architecture describes the interconnected structure through which political, economic, security, scientific, communications, navigation, and information systems rely on satellites and their supporting terrestrial infrastructure.

Communications, positioning, timing, Earth observation, weather systems, constellations, launch infrastructure, launch vehicles, satellite manufacturing, components, ground stations, data infrastructure, spectrum, terminals, supply chains, and technical expertise form overlapping dependency layers.

Satellite and space dependency can be technological, industrial, infrastructural, geographic, institutional, concentrated, distributed, direct, or embedded within terrestrial systems.

The existence of satellite and space dependency does not by itself establish vulnerability, political control, coercion, service disruption, strategic dominance, or a specific political outcome.

Satellite and space dependency architecture therefore requires continuous separation of observable structural reliance from interpretation.
