### DGCP™ Politics Shot #2551: Standards & Regulatory Dependency

Standards and regulatory dependency exists when participation in political, economic, industrial, technological, or infrastructure systems relies on defined technical, legal, administrative, or institutional requirements.

Possessing physical or technical capability does not necessarily establish permission or compatibility to participate in a wider system.

Standards and regulatory structures can connect products, organizations, infrastructure, markets, and jurisdictions through shared requirements.

Standards and regulatory dependency therefore forms an institutional layer across multiple systems.
