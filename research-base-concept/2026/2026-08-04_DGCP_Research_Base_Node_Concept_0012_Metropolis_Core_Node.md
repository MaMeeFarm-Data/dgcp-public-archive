# DGCP™ Research Base / Node Concept #0012

## Metropolis Core Node

### Urban Systems Research Base

**Record ID:** RNC-000012

---

## Concept Statement

> Observe the city.  
> Understand complexity.  
> Respect the people.  
> Design for a livable future.

DGCP™ Research Base / Node Concept #0012 explores a long-term urban research base located conceptually within the core of a major global metropolis.

The concept investigates how cities, people, infrastructure, mobility, energy, digital systems, governance, public space, and innovation interact within dense metropolitan environments.

Rather than separating research from the city, the node is positioned directly inside the urban system being observed.

---

## Reference Environment

**Reference Location**

Global Metropolitan Central Business District

Conceptual references include dense international urban environments such as:

- Bangkok
- Singapore
- Tokyo
- Hong Kong

Conceptual characteristics include:

- high-density urban development
- mass transit connectivity
- mixed land use
- diverse communities
- business and innovation networks
- public spaces
- digital infrastructure
- complex mobility systems

The location represents a reference environment only and does not identify a confirmed property or development site.

---

## Research Role

Primary research themes include:

- urban systems observation
- mobility and flow research
- energy and resource efficiency
- data and digital infrastructure
- social behavior and well-being
- governance and policy
- resilience and adaptation
- long-term evidence preservation

---

## Architectural Concept

The architectural language explores a vertical research environment integrated into a dense metropolitan setting.

Design principles include:

- vertical living and working
- open and transparent spaces
- natural light
- urban greenery
- efficient building systems
- flexible collaboration spaces
- people-centered design
- reduced environmental footprint

The concept treats the building as both a research environment and an operational component within the surrounding city.

---

## Master Plan

Because the node is vertical, the conceptual master plan is organized primarily through floors, terraces, circulation, and shared operational zones rather than a conventional ground-based campus.

Conceptual facilities include:

- Research Floor
- Collaboration Hub
- Data & Analysis Center
- Library & Knowledge Hub
- Meeting & Seminar Room
- Outdoor Sky Terrace
- Urban Garden
- Wellness & Meditation Space
- Cafe & Social Area
- Service & Support Core
- Energy & Utility Zone
- Emergency Exit / Refuge

## Mobility & Connectivity

The node is designed conceptually around strong integration with metropolitan mobility networks.

Potential connectivity includes:

- MRT / BTS / LRT systems
- pedestrian networks
- bicycle infrastructure
- electric vehicle charging
- smart parking
- logistics and service access

Urban accessibility is treated as part of the research environment rather than as a separate infrastructure layer.
