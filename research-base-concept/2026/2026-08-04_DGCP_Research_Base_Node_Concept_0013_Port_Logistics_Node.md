# DGCP™ Research Base / Node Concept #0013

## Port Logistics Node

### Coastal Logistics & Supply Chain Research Base

**Record ID:** RNC-000013

---

## Concept Statement

> Observe the flow.  
> Understand the system.  
> Respect the movement.  
> Design for seamless connection.  
> Build for the future.

DGCP™ Research Base / Node Concept #0013 explores a long-term research base positioned conceptually within a coastal port and logistics environment.

The concept examines maritime connectivity, logistics systems, trade flows, multimodal transport, infrastructure, operational efficiency, energy, data, and resilience within an integrated port research environment.

The node is positioned close to active logistics systems so that movement, connections, infrastructure, and operational patterns can be observed within their wider systems context.
