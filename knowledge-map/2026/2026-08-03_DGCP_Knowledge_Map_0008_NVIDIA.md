## Purpose

This educational knowledge map presents a structural overview of NVIDIA and illustrates how the company connects graphics processing, accelerated computing, artificial intelligence, data center infrastructure, software, high-performance computing, and developer ecosystems.

The objective is to simplify NVIDIA's technology ecosystem into an accessible visual learning resource.

## Core Learning Areas

### Graphics & Accelerated Computing

NVIDIA's computing platform developed from graphics processing technologies and expanded into accelerated computing applications across multiple technical domains.

Key technologies represented in the map include:

- GPUs
- GeForce
- RTX
- Real-Time Ray Tracing
- DLSS

---

### AI Computing Platform

NVIDIA provides computing technologies supporting artificial intelligence workloads through components including:

- CUDA
- Tensor Cores
- NVIDIA DGX
- AI Supercomputers
- Software Ecosystem

The combination of hardware and software creates an integrated platform for accelerated AI computing.

---

### Data Center Infrastructure

NVIDIA technologies are used in data center environments supporting AI models and high-performance workloads.

The map highlights:

- H100 / H200 GPUs
- High-Performance GPUs
- Scalable Infrastructure
- NVLink Networking
- AI Cloud Services

---

### AI Software & Ecosystem

NVIDIA extends beyond semiconductor hardware through a software ecosystem that includes:

- NVIDIA AI Enterprise
- Omniverse
- Isaac Robotics
- NeMo
- NGC Catalog

This software layer connects computing infrastructure with AI development and deployment.

---

### Automotive & Robotics

NVIDIA develops computing platforms for automotive and robotics applications, including:

- DRIVE Platform
- Autonomous Vehicle Computing
- AI Cockpit Technologies
- Robotics Solutions
- Simulation

---

### High-Performance Computing

Accelerated computing technologies support applications such as:

- Scientific Computing
- Climate Modeling
- Digital Twins
- HPC Workloads
- Accelerated Discovery

---

### Developer Ecosystem

Developers form an important layer of the NVIDIA ecosystem through CUDA adoption, libraries, SDKs, training resources, and software development communities.

The relationship between hardware, software, developers, AI models, applications, partners, and data centers extends the platform beyond individual computing components.

---

## Educational Perspective

This knowledge map demonstrates how NVIDIA connects semiconductor architecture, accelerated computing, AI infrastructure, software platforms, developer tools, data centers, robotics, and industry applications into a broader computing ecosystem.

It emphasizes observable structural relationships between technology layers and ecosystem participants rather than investment judgment, prediction, or commercial recommendation.
