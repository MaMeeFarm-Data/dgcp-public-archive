## Meta

## Purpose

This educational knowledge map presents a structural overview of Meta and illustrates how the company connects social platforms, digital advertising, artificial intelligence, immersive technologies, creator tools, data infrastructure, and global digital communities.

The objective is to simplify Meta's technology and platform ecosystem into an accessible visual learning resource.

---

The map explores:

- Social Connection

- Advertising Platform
- Metaverse Vision
- AI & Technology
- Content & Discovery
- Privacy & Safety
- Economy & Creator Support
- Connectivity & Infrastructure
- Data & Analytics
- Social Impact
- Global Presence
- Innovation Culture
- Sustainability

## Core Learning Areas

### Social Platform Ecosystem

Meta operates digital platforms that support communication, content sharing, communities, and social interaction.

The ecosystem represented in the map includes:

- Facebook
- Instagram
- WhatsApp
- Messenger
- Threads

Together, these platforms connect users, communities, creators, businesses, and other ecosystem participants.

---

### Advertising Platform

Digital advertising forms an important component of Meta's platform architecture.

The map highlights:

- Meta Ads Manager
- Audience Targeting
- Advertising Formats
- Measurement Tools
- Business Solutions

These systems connect businesses with audiences across Meta's digital platforms.

---

### Artificial Intelligence & Technology

Meta develops and applies technologies including:

- Artificial Intelligence
- Machine Learning
- Computer Vision
- Natural Language Technologies
- AI Infrastructure

AI capabilities support multiple layers of the company's applications, infrastructure, and research activities.

---

### Immersive Technology

Meta's technology ecosystem also includes development in:

- Virtual Reality
- Augmented Reality
- Spatial Computing
- Immersive Experiences

These technologies represent another computing interface within the broader Meta ecosystem.

---

### Content & Creator Ecosystem

Meta provides systems supporting content discovery and digital creators through features such as:

- Feeds
- Reels
- Stories
- Discovery Systems
- Creator Tools
- Monetization Tools
- Subscriptions
- Digital Commerce

These components connect content creation, distribution, discovery, audiences, and economic activity.

---

### Connectivity & Infrastructure

Large-scale digital platforms depend on underlying infrastructure.

The map identifies structural components including:

- Data Centers
- Network Infrastructure
- Internet Connectivity
- Low-Latency Systems

These infrastructure layers support communication and digital services across the platform ecosystem.

---

### Data & Analytics

Meta uses data and analytics capabilities for areas including:

- Data Insights
- Analytics Tools
- Audience Insights
- Attribution
- Platform Measurement

These systems support observation and operation across large-scale digital platforms.

---

## Educational Perspective

This knowledge map demonstrates how Meta combines social platforms, advertising systems, artificial intelligence, digital content, immersive technology, infrastructure, data, creators, businesses, and communities into an interconnected technology ecosystem.

It emphasizes structural relationships among platform components and ecosystem participants rather than commercial recommendation, prediction, or judgment.
