## Purpose

This educational knowledge map presents a structural overview of Samsung and illustrates how the company connects semiconductors, mobile devices, displays, consumer electronics, home appliances, research, manufacturing, and global operations.

The objective is to simplify the broader Samsung technology ecosystem into an accessible visual learning resource.

## Core Learning Areas

### Diverse Businesses

Samsung operates across multiple technology and electronics domains.

The map represents areas including:

- Mobile Experience
- Device Solutions
- Display
- Consumer Electronics
- Home Appliances

This portfolio connects component technologies with finished products and broader consumer ecosystems.

---

### Semiconductor Technology

Semiconductors form an important technology layer within the Samsung ecosystem.

The map highlights:

- Advanced Semiconductors
- Memory Technology
- Foundry Operations
- AI & On-Device Intelligence
- Connectivity Technologies

These capabilities connect semiconductor manufacturing with devices, computing, communications, and other technology markets.

---

### Product Ecosystem

Samsung's product ecosystem includes:

- Galaxy Smartphones
- Tablets & Wearables
- TVs & Audio
- Home Appliances
- SmartThings

These product categories create connections among hardware, software, connectivity, and user environments.

---

### Research & Development

Research and development supports multiple parts of the company's technology portfolio.

The map represents:

- Global R&D Networks
- Innovation Centers
- Future Technologies
- Open Collaboration
- Patents & Intellectual Property

R&D connects technology development with products, components, manufacturing, and longer-term capabilities.

---

### Global Operations

Samsung operates through an international structure involving manufacturing, distribution, partnerships, research, and local market activities.

The map highlights the relationship between global operations and localized execution.

---

### Partnerships

The wider ecosystem includes relationships with:

- Suppliers
- Carrier & Operator Partners
- Research Institutions
- Technology Partners
- Startup Ecosystems

These relationships connect Samsung's internal capabilities with external organizations and infrastructure.

---

### Quality & Reliability

Product quality, safety standards, customer satisfaction, continuous improvement, and reliability are represented as operational components within the wider system.

These functions connect product development with manufacturing, distribution, and customer use.

---

## Educational Perspective

This knowledge map demonstrates how Samsung connects semiconductors, devices, displays, consumer electronics, research, manufacturing, products, people, and partnerships within a large-scale global technology ecosystem.

It emphasizes structural relationships among these components rather than prediction, commercial recommendation, or judgment.
