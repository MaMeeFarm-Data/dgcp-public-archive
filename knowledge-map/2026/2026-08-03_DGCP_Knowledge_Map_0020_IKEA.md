## Purpose

This educational knowledge map presents a structural overview of IKEA and illustrates how design, affordability, global operations, suppliers, customers, resources, people, and partners interact within a large home-furnishing ecosystem.

The objective is to simplify the wider IKEA system into an accessible visual learning resource.

---

## Knowledge Overview

The map explores:

- Vision & Mission
- Global Presence
- Affordable Solutions
- Design & Innovation
- Sustainability
- Customer Experience
- Economic Impact
- Resource Use
- People & Culture
- Quality & Reliability
- Community Impact
- Partners & Stakeholders
- The Future
- Core Values

---

## Core Learning Areas

### Global Presence

IKEA operates through a distributed international ecosystem connecting stores, digital channels, customers, suppliers, employees, and business partners.

The map illustrates how global scale interacts with local operations and customer environments.

---

### Affordable Solutions

Affordability is represented through relationships among:

- Product Design
- Functional Solutions
- Compact Packaging
- Flat-Pack Systems
- Customer Assembly
- Cost Efficiency

These components illustrate how product architecture and operational design can interact with pricing and distribution.

---

### Design & Innovation

The map highlights design as a structural component connecting functionality, simplicity, materials, manufacturing, packaging, and customer experience.

Innovation therefore extends beyond individual products into the wider operating system.

---

### Customer Experience

The customer layer includes:

- Online & In-Store Shopping
- Product Inspiration
- Delivery & Assembly
- After-Sales Support
- Accessible Product Solutions

These interactions connect physical stores, digital channels, logistics, products, and services.

---

### Quality & Reliability

The map represents quality through:

- Safety Testing
- Material Quality
- Durability
- Reliability
- Customer Satisfaction

Quality therefore interacts with product design, sourcing, production, and customer use.

---

### Resource Use & Sustainability

Resource efficiency and sustainability interact with several operational areas.

The map highlights:

- Efficient Resource Use
- Renewable Energy
- Waste Reduction
- Water Stewardship
- Sustainable Materials
- Circular Economy
- Responsible Sourcing

These elements demonstrate relationships between material flows, operations, products, and environmental considerations.

---

### People & Culture

Human capability remains part of the wider system.

The map represents:

- Team Structure
- Diversity & Inclusion
- Learning & Growth
- Health & Well-Being
- Employee Participation

People interact with stores, operations, customers, suppliers, and organizational processes.

---

### Partners & Stakeholders

The IKEA ecosystem connects multiple participants, including:

- Suppliers
- Business Partners
- Communities
- Customers
- Employees
- Shareholders

These relationships illustrate how a large retail and furnishing system depends on interconnected stakeholders.

---

## Educational Perspective

This knowledge map demonstrates how IKEA connects design, products, affordability, stores, digital channels, suppliers, customers, employees, resources, logistics, partners, and communities within a global ecosystem.

It emphasizes observable structural relationships among system participants and operational components rather than commercial judgment, prediction, or investment analysis.
