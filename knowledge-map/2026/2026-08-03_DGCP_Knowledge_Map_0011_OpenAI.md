## Purpose

This educational knowledge map presents a structural overview of OpenAI and illustrates how the organization connects artificial intelligence research, models, products, developer platforms, safety work, infrastructure, partnerships, and deployment.

The objective is to simplify the broader AI ecosystem into an accessible visual learning resource.

---

## Core Learning Areas

### AI Models

OpenAI develops artificial intelligence models designed to work across different forms of information and tasks.

The visual map represents model development as a foundational layer connecting research with products, applications, and developer tools.

---

### Products & Platforms

The OpenAI ecosystem includes user-facing products and developer platforms represented in the map through:

- ChatGPT
- API Platform
- Playground
- Developer APIs
- Fine-tuning
- Model-based tools

These components provide different interfaces for individuals, developers, and organizations to interact with AI capabilities.

---

### Research

Research forms a central part of the ecosystem.

Areas represented in the map include:

- Frontier AI Research
- Alignment & Safety
- Robustness & Reliability
- Reasoning & Planning
- Multimodal Intelligence

Research capabilities connect model development with evaluation, deployment, and longer-term technical objectives.

---

### Technology Stack

The map presents several technical layers associated with modern AI systems, including:

- Large Language Models
- Reinforcement Learning
- Supervised Learning
- Self-Supervised Learning
- Tool Use & Agents
- Scalable Infrastructure

These layers interact across model development, training, evaluation, and deployment.

---

### Developer Ecosystem

Developers interact with the platform through APIs, SDKs, libraries, documentation, examples, and development tools.

This creates an ecosystem connecting AI models with external software, applications, businesses, and technical experimentation.

---

### Safety & Alignment

The map identifies safety and alignment as structural components of AI development, including:

- Safety Research
- Model Evaluation
- Red Teaming
- Bias & Fairness
- Transparency
- Human Oversight

These activities connect model development with evaluation and deployment processes.

---

### Partnerships & Infrastructure

AI development and deployment depend on relationships involving computing infrastructure, research collaboration, technology partners, and developer ecosystems.

The map highlights these relationships as part of the wider organizational structure rather than treating models as isolated components.

---

## Educational Perspective

This knowledge map demonstrates how OpenAI connects research, AI models, products, developer platforms, safety processes, infrastructure, partnerships, and users within a broader artificial intelligence ecosystem.

It emphasizes structural relationships among these components rather than prediction, commercial recommendation, or judgment.
