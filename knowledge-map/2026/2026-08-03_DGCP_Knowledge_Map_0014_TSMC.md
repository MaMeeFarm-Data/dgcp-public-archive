## Purpose

This educational knowledge map presents a structural overview of TSMC and illustrates how the company connects semiconductor foundry operations, advanced process technology, global manufacturing, research and development, customers, suppliers, and technology ecosystems.

The objective is to simplify the broader semiconductor foundry system into an accessible visual learning resource.

## Core Learning Areas

### Foundry Model

TSMC operates a dedicated semiconductor foundry model focused on manufacturing chips designed by customers.

The map highlights structural characteristics including:

- Pure-Play Foundry Model
- Advanced Process Technology
- Deep Manufacturing Expertise
- Production Execution
- Reliability

This model connects semiconductor designers with large-scale manufacturing capability.

---

### Advanced Semiconductor Technology

Advanced process technology forms a central layer of the TSMC ecosystem.

The map represents areas including:

- Advanced Process Nodes
- Extreme Ultraviolet Lithography
- Advanced Packaging
- Design Enablement
- Continuous Technology Development

These capabilities support semiconductor applications across multiple computing and electronics markets.

---

### Customer Ecosystem

TSMC's manufacturing capabilities connect with customers operating across areas such as:

- AI & High-Performance Computing
- Smartphones
- Automotive
- IoT & Edge Computing
- Data Centers
- Communications

This customer structure illustrates the foundry's position within wider technology supply chains.

---

### Global Manufacturing

Semiconductor production depends on highly specialized fabrication infrastructure and interconnected supply networks.

The map highlights manufacturing operations, geographic expansion, local partnerships, and supply-chain diversification as components of TSMC's global production structure.

---

### Research & Development

Research and development supports continued advancement in semiconductor manufacturing.

The map represents:

- Long-Term Technology Roadmaps
- Process Development
- Collaborative Innovation
- Global Technical Talent
- Continuous Improvement

R&D connects future process capabilities with manufacturing and customer requirements.

---

### Partnerships & Supply Ecosystem

TSMC operates within a wider semiconductor ecosystem involving:

- EDA & IP Partners
- Equipment Suppliers
- Material Suppliers
- Research Institutions
- Government & Academia

These relationships demonstrate that advanced semiconductor production depends on multiple specialized systems operating together.

---

### Quality & Reliability

Quality control, operational stability, business continuity, and manufacturing reliability are important structural components of semiconductor production.

These capabilities connect fabrication processes with customer requirements and wider technology supply chains.

---

## Educational Perspective

This knowledge map demonstrates how TSMC connects advanced semiconductor processes, fabrication infrastructure, customers, suppliers, research, manufacturing, and global technology systems within an interconnected foundry ecosystem.

It emphasizes structural relationships among these components rather than prediction, investment recommendation, or commercial judgment.
