### DGCP™ Market Scan #2656: Vegetable Oil Markets

Vegetable oil markets connect agricultural production, oil bearing crops, extraction, refining, storage, transportation, trade, food manufacturing, industrial demand, and energy systems.

Different vegetable oils originate from different crops, production geographies, processing structures, and downstream applications.

The market therefore consists of interconnected but differentiated commodity systems.

I don't see vegetable oils operating through one uniform market.
