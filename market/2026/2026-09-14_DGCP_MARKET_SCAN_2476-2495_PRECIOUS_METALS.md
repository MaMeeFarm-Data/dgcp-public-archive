### DGCP™ Market Scan #2476: Precious Metals Market

Precious metals markets connect geological resources, mining, refining, fabrication, recycling, inventories, trade, pricing, industrial demand, and stores of value.

Gold, silver, platinum group metals, and related materials move through different physical and commercial systems.

The category therefore contains multiple metals with distinct supply structures and downstream functions.

I don't see precious metals operating as one uniform market.

### DGCP™ Market Scan #2477: Precious Metal Resources

Precious metal resources occur across geological deposits with different grades, mineral associations, depths, locations, and extraction conditions.

Commercial accessibility depends on geology, technology, infrastructure, capital, energy, water, regulation, and processing capability.

Resource presence does not automatically translate into available refined metal.

I don't see resource conditions becoming uniform.

Precious metal reserves represent identified resources considered recoverable under defined technical and economic conditions.

Reserve classifications interact with geological knowledge, mine design, ore grade, processing methods, infrastructure, costs, and commercial viability.

Reported reserves remain distinct from immediate market supply.

I don't see that distinction disappearing.

### DGCP™ Market Scan #2479: Precious Metal Mining

Precious metal mining extracts ores containing economically recoverable concentrations of valuable metals.

Production depends on deposit characteristics, mine development, equipment, workforce, energy, water, logistics, and processing access.

### DGCP™ Market Scan #2480: Precious Metal Ore Grade

Precious metal ore grade influences how much material must be extracted and processed to recover a given quantity of metal.

Grade interacts with mining methods, energy use, processing requirements, waste handling, recovery systems, and operating economics.

Equivalent ore volumes therefore do not represent equivalent metal content.

I don't see ore grade losing structural relevance.

I log precious metal ore grade.

### DGCP™ Market Scan #2481: Precious Metal Processing

Precious metal processing separates valuable metals from mined ores and concentrates before final refining.

Processing requirements vary according to mineralogy, grade, associated metals, recovery technology, energy, water, reagents, and product specifications.

This stage connects mine output with refined material supply.

I don't see processing becoming structurally secondary.

I log precious metal processing.

Precious metal refining produces metals with defined purity and specifications for downstream markets.

Refining depends on suitable feedstock, specialized facilities, technology, quality control, energy, security, and operating capability.
