### DGCP™ Market Scan #2356: Aluminum Market

Aluminum markets connect mineral resources, bauxite mining, alumina refining, smelting, fabrication, recycling, trade, pricing, and consumption.

Physical aluminum moves through multiple resource and industrial stages before entering transportation, construction, packaging, electrical, machinery, and manufacturing systems.

The market therefore connects mineral supply with energy intensive industrial capacity.

I don’t see structural relevance declining.

### DGCP™ Market Scan #2357: Bauxite Resources

Bauxite resources occur across geological deposits with different grades, mineral compositions, locations, and extraction conditions.

Commercial accessibility depends on geology, mine development, infrastructure, capital, regulation, logistics, and processing access.

Resource presence does not automatically translate into available aluminum supply.

I don’t see resource conditions becoming uniform.

I log bauxite resources.

### DGCP™ Market Scan #2358: Bauxite Mining

Bauxite mining extracts aluminum bearing ore for downstream processing into alumina.

Production depends on mine development, equipment, workforce, land access, transportation, operating capability, and ore characteristics.

Mining forms the upstream physical layer of the primary aluminum supply chain.

I don’t see extraction becoming sufficient on its own.

I log bauxite mining.

### DGCP™ Market Scan #2359: Bauxite Quality

Bauxite quality varies according to alumina content, silica, mineral composition, moisture, and other physical characteristics.

Ore characteristics influence refining requirements, material handling, processing efficiency, and downstream economics.

Bauxite therefore does not function as a completely uniform raw material.

I don’t see quality distinctions disappearing.

I log bauxite quality.

### DGCP™ Market Scan #2360: Bauxite Trade

Bauxite trade connects mining regions with alumina refineries across domestic and international supply networks.

Physical flows depend on mine output, transport infrastructure, ports, vessels, refinery demand, contracts, and ore characteristics.

Geographic separation between mining and refining creates an international raw material market.

I don’t see trade geography becoming irrelevant.

I log bauxite trade.

### DGCP™ Market Scan #2361: Alumina Refining

Alumina refining converts bauxite into aluminum oxide suitable for primary aluminum production.

Refining depends on bauxite supply, processing facilities, energy, water, chemicals, infrastructure, waste management, and operating capability.

This stage forms a distinct industrial layer between mineral extraction and metal production.

I don’t see alumina refining becoming structurally secondary.

I log alumina refining.

### DGCP™ Market Scan #2362: Alumina Market

Alumina markets connect bauxite producers and refineries with primary aluminum smelters.

Supply depends on refinery capacity, feedstock availability, energy, logistics, inventories, contracts, and trade access.

Alumina therefore operates as an intermediate commodity within the aluminum system.

I don’t see this market layer disappearing.

I log alumina markets.

### DGCP™ Market Scan #2363: Primary Aluminum Smelting

Primary aluminum smelting converts alumina into metallic aluminum through specialized electrochemical processing.

Smelters depend on alumina, electricity, carbon materials, equipment, workforce, infrastructure, and continuous operating capability.

Metal production therefore requires substantial industrial capacity beyond mineral extraction.

I don’t see smelting becoming structurally optional.

I log primary aluminum smelting.

---

### DGCP™ Market Scan #2364: Aluminum Smelting Capacity

Aluminum smelting capacity defines the ability of operating facilities to convert alumina into primary metal.

Usable capacity depends on electricity availability, alumina supply, equipment condition, maintenance, operating economics, workforce, and infrastructure.

Installed smelting capacity does not automatically equal continuously available production.

I don’t see that distinction disappearing.

I log aluminum smelting capacity.

### DGCP™ Market Scan #2365: Electricity and Aluminum Production

Electricity forms a major operational input into primary aluminum smelting.

Smelter output depends on access to large and relatively continuous electricity supply alongside other production inputs.

The aluminum system therefore connects mineral markets directly with power infrastructure.

I don’t see that dependency becoming secondary.

I log electricity and aluminum production.

### DGCP™ Market Scan #2366: Primary Aluminum Market

Primary aluminum markets connect smelters with traders, warehouses, fabricators, manufacturers, and industrial consumers.

Metal moves through physical and commercial networks in standardized and specialized forms.

Primary supply represents one layer within the broader aluminum material system.

I don’t see that market operating independently.

I log primary aluminum markets.

### DGCP™ Market Scan #2367: Aluminum Fabrication

Aluminum fabrication converts primary and recycled metal into sheet, plate, foil, extrusion, wire, cast products, and other industrial forms.

Fabrication connects commodity metal with transportation, buildings, packaging, electrical equipment, machinery, and consumer products.

Usable industrial supply therefore extends beyond primary metal production.

I don’t see fabrication losing its system role.

I log aluminum fabrication.

### DGCP™ Market Scan #2368: Aluminum Rolling

Aluminum rolling converts metal into sheet, plate, and foil used across packaging, transportation, construction, industrial, and other applications.

Rolling depends on suitable metal input, specialized mills, energy, technical capability, and product specifications.

Downstream capacity determines which forms of aluminum can enter particular markets.

I don’t see rolling becoming structurally secondary.

I log aluminum rolling.

### DGCP™ Market Scan #2369: Aluminum Extrusion

Aluminum extrusion forms metal into profiles and shapes used across construction, transportation, machinery, electrical, and manufacturing systems.

Production depends on alloy selection, billet supply, presses, tooling, energy, finishing, and technical requirements.

Commodity metal therefore passes through additional transformation before many end uses.

I don’t see extrusion losing industrial relevance.

I log aluminum extrusion.

### DGCP™ Market Scan #2370: Aluminum Recycling

Aluminum recycling recovers metal from manufacturing scrap, packaging, vehicles, buildings, equipment, and other material streams.

Recovered aluminum can return to melting, alloying, casting, rolling, extrusion, and manufacturing systems.

Secondary supply therefore forms an integrated component of the broader aluminum market.

I don’t see recycling becoming detached from primary supply.

I log aluminum recycling.

### DGCP™ Market Scan #2371: Aluminum Inventories

Aluminum inventories provide physical buffering between smelting, trading, fabrication, manufacturing, and consumption.

Stocks can exist at producers, warehouses, traders, fabricators, manufacturers, and other locations.

Inventory location, form, and specification influence how material can move through the market.

I don’t see inventories becoming structurally uniform.

I log aluminum inventories.

Aluminum trade flows connect bauxite mines, alumina refineries, smelters, fabricators, recycling systems, and consuming markets across geographic boundaries.

Material moves internationally as ore, alumina, primary metal, semi fabricated products, finished goods, and scrap.
