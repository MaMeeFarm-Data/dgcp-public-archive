### DGCP™ Market Scan #2276: Refined Petroleum Products

Refined petroleum product markets connect crude oil supply, refining, storage, transportation, distribution, trade, pricing, and consumption.

Product markets remain connected with both upstream crude supply and downstream demand structures.

### DGCP™ Market Scan #2278: Refinery Capacity

Refinery capacity defines the volume of crude oil that refining systems can process over a given period.

Usable capacity depends on refinery configuration, maintenance, operating rates, crude availability, utilities, and downstream product demand.

Installed capacity does not automatically equal continuously available processing capacity.

I don’t see that distinction disappearing.
