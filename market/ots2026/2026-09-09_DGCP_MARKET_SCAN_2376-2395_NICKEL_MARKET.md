### DGCP™ Market Scan #2376: Nickel Market

Nickel markets connect geological resources, mining, processing, refining, intermediate products, alloys, batteries, recycling, trade, pricing, and consumption.

Operations interact with ore grade, mineral composition, excavation, transport, moisture, processing technology, energy, and infrastructure.

Laterite resources can feed multiple processing routes depending on ore characteristics and intended products.
